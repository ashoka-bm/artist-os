# Form-sensitive guidance

Clarity and concision must serve the approved text form.

## Strong fit

Clear Writing Pass usually helps:

- essays,
- artist statements,
- professional letters,
- treatments,
- outlines,
- articles,
- explanatory prose,
- public-facing guidance,
- social posts where directness matters.

## Conditional fit

Use carefully for:

- monologues,
- scripts,
- manifestos,
- source adaptations,
- prose scenes.

Protect voice, pacing, character logic, rhetorical force, and source wording.

## Weak fit

Usually skip or use only light edits for:

- poems,
- lyrics,
- ritual text,
- experimental prose,
- deliberately fragmented work.

In these forms, ambiguity, repetition, silence, and uneven rhythm may be the structure.
