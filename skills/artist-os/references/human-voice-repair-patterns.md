# Voice repair patterns

Use these moves to repair AI-sounding text without changing the approved work.

## Prefer concrete detail

Replace abstract significance with observable detail, action, image, or consequence.

Weak:

> This marks a pivotal moment in the journey.

Stronger:

> This is the first time the speaker admits they wanted to leave.

## Use simple verbs

Prefer direct verbs when ornate substitutes add nothing:

- is,
- are,
- has,
- says,
- shows,
- changes,
- breaks,
- returns.

Avoid changing a deliberate elevated style in poems, manifestos, or ritual text.

## Vary rhythm

Human prose does not keep the same sentence shape for long. Mix short sentences with longer ones when the form permits it.

Do not manufacture messiness. Forced casualness is another AI tell.

## Keep the author's stance

Do not add opinions the artist did not authorize. If the approved voice is uncertain, let uncertainty remain precise rather than generic.

Weak:

> This is concerning.

Stronger:

> The unsettling part is that the speaker notices the door only after deciding not to leave.

## Cut generic endings

Replace broad uplifting conclusions with the actual final pressure, image, or decision in the piece.

## Repair lists

If a list contains non-parallel ideas, turn it into prose or split it into separate moves. If the list is structurally useful, keep it.

## Preserve intentional form

Before editing, identify whether repetition, symmetry, fragmentation, line breaks, or heightened diction are doing artistic work. Protected form beats pattern cleanup.
