# Clear writing anti-patterns

Use this list to diagnose clarity problems.

## Paragraphs with no job

A paragraph fails when it restates the previous one, gestures at importance, or adds context without changing what the reader understands.

## Topic drift

If the paragraph starts on one claim and ends on another, either revise the transition or split the paragraph.

## Abstract stacking

Too many abstract nouns in a row make prose hard to feel:

- transformation,
- significance,
- complexity,
- experience,
- relationship,
- process,
- impact.

Replace with the actual image, action, decision, or consequence.

## False parallelism

Lists and repeated structures work only when items are truly parallel. Otherwise use prose.

## Weak transitions

Avoid transitions that only gesture:

- furthermore,
- additionally,
- moreover,
- in conclusion,
- it is important to note.

Use a transition that names the actual relation: contrast, cause, consequence, exception, escalation, return.

## Buried actor

If the sentence hides who acted, clarify unless concealment is intentional.

## Over-compression

Concision can damage meaning. Do not cut examples, sensory details, emotional turns, or pauses that the Text Generation Plan requires.

## Generic summary ending

End with the actual final movement, not a summary that sounds like a report closing.
