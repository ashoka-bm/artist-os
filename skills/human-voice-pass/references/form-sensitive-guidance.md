# Form-sensitive guidance

The Human Voice Pass must serve the text form. Do not apply ordinary prose standards blindly.

## Essay, article, artist statement, letter, treatment

Usually safe to remove AI vocabulary, filler, generic conclusions, fake balance, and over-polished sentence rhythm.

Prefer specificity, paragraph force, and a voice that sounds like a person making choices.

## Poem

Protect line breaks, repetition, compression, silence, ambiguity, and strange diction when they carry the poem's meaning.

Do not normalize syntax just because it is unusual.

## Lyrics

Protect hook, meter, rhyme, singability, section function, refrain, and mouth feel.

Do not replace repeated phrases if repetition is musical.

## Script and dialogue

Protect character voice, subtext, interruption, unfinished sentences, and speech rhythm.

Do not make every line clear, balanced, or grammatically complete.

## Monologue

Protect the speaker's psychology. Unevenness may be the point.

Clean AI artifacts, but do not flatten obsession, contradiction, or drift if the Text Generation Plan calls for it.

## Manifesto

Heightened rhetoric may be intentional. Remove generic AI slogans, but preserve force.

## Source-preserving adaptation

Source-wording policy wins. Do not humanize by paraphrasing protected source language.

## Experimental prose or ritual text

Artificiality may be deliberate. Only remove patterns the Text Generation Plan identifies as unwanted.
