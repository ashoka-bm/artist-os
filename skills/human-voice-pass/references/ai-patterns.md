# AI-writing patterns

Use this reference to identify common AI-writing tells. Do not treat it as a banned-word list. A phrase is a problem only when it weakens the approved text form, voice, or meaning.

## Inflated significance

Watch for phrases that imply importance without evidence:

- stands as,
- serves as,
- testament to,
- pivotal moment,
- crucial role,
- key moment,
- lasting impact,
- evolving landscape,
- setting the stage,
- deeply rooted.

Replace with the concrete fact, image, action, or claim.

## Superficial analysis

Remove or rewrite trailing clauses that sound analytical but add little:

- highlighting,
- emphasizing,
- underscoring,
- ensuring,
- reflecting,
- showcasing,
- contributing to,
- fostering,
- symbolizing.

## Promotional language

Avoid generic praise unless the text form intentionally uses promotion:

- vibrant,
- rich,
- profound,
- breathtaking,
- renowned,
- groundbreaking,
- stunning,
- nestled,
- in the heart of.

## Vague attribution

Replace vague sources with concrete ones when factual support matters:

- experts argue,
- observers have cited,
- industry reports,
- several sources,
- some critics argue.

If the packet does not authorize adding facts, remove the vague claim or flag it.

## AI vocabulary clusters

Watch for clustering of:

- additionally,
- delve,
- tapestry,
- landscape as an abstract noun,
- pivotal,
- underscore,
- showcase,
- intricate,
- interplay,
- foster,
- garner,
- enhance,
- valuable,
- robust,
- seamless.

One word may be fine. Several in a paragraph usually signals generic AI prose.

## Formulaic structures

Watch for:

- "not just X, but Y",
- "not only X, but also Y",
- forced rule-of-three lists,
- false ranges using "from X to Y" without real endpoints,
- repeated "X but not Y" contrasts,
- "on one hand / on the other hand" when the text does not need courtroom balance.

## Assistant artifacts

Remove conversational assistant residue unless the artifact intentionally includes it:

- Of course,
- Certainly,
- Great question,
- I hope this helps,
- Let me know,
- Here is,
- As of my last update.

## Performed authenticity

Second-generation AI text often performs humanness. Watch for:

- "And honestly?",
- "Maybe both.",
- "Maybe that's the point.",
- "I think that says something.",
- parenthetical personality injections,
- philosophical mic-drop endings,
- brand-manifesto paragraph shapes where each paragraph is one neat abstract value.

Repair by returning to specific detail, uneven but purposeful rhythm, and the approved voice.
