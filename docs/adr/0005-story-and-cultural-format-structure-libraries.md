# Story And Cultural Format Structure Libraries

Status: accepted; hardened after Story Structure rehearsals.

Artist OS separates **Story Structure Library** from **Cultural Format Structure Library**. Story Structure names the deep emotional, symbolic, narrative, rhetorical, or experiential movement and is adapted inside the Beat Plan; Cultural Format Structure names the culturally recognized audience-facing grammar of a form and is adapted inside the Medium Plan after Medium Output Shape Recommendation.

This keeps story movement from becoming a rigid format template while still letting outputs honor culturally legible forms such as articles, three-act plots, feature essays, scripts, trailers, and hook-driven posts. Plot-tracker-style documents are **Stewardship Views** over Medium Plan structure and Long-Work Stewardship state, not new story-authority or tracking records.

## Consequences

- Adapted Story Structure is now required in Beat Plan for non-`single_beat` story modes: `beat_pair`, `triptych`, `sequence`, `scene`, `arc`, and `world`.
- Adapted Story Structure remains optional for `single_beat` Beat Plans.
- Medium Output Shape Recommendation and adapted Cultural Format Structure remain text-first Text Medium Plan fields while cross-medium behavior is rehearsed.
- Cross-medium adoption for image, sound, video, and mixed media remains a follow-up decision after the Text Journey rehearsal hardens the fields.
- Structure libraries should be research-informed, but library entries stay concise and operational rather than carrying citation lists.
