# Draft Pattern: Pipeline-Driven Video Production

Status: research draft.

OpenMontage shows a post-storyboard pattern where the agent does not jump from storyboard to prompt. It first selects a production pipeline with known stages, artifacts, tools, approvals, and review criteria.

## Draft Principle

After storyboard approval, Artist OS may need a production route before any provider export. The route should state the output family, required artifacts, available tools, approval points, cost path, and validation gates.

## Common Stages

- Research or reference analysis.
- Proposal with concept, capability, budget, and runtime choices.
- Script or narration plan.
- Scene plan or storyboard alignment.
- Asset manifest.
- Edit decisions.
- Compose or render.
- Final review.
- Publish package.

## Why This Matters

Provider prompt packs mostly answer "what prompt do I paste?" Pipeline-driven production answers "what kind of video are we making, with what materials, what renderer, what cost, and what quality gate?"

## Artist OS Mapping

This should sit after the Video Storyboard. The Video Medium Plan remains the planning authority. A production route can translate the approved storyboard into the downstream assembly path without changing Artist Meaning, Beat Plan, or Video Style Expression.
