# Draft Pattern: Compact Multi-Shot Export

Status: research draft.

Some animation providers benefit from a compact, timecoded prompt rather than a full storyboard document. The compact multi-shot export condenses the approved Video Medium Plan into short shot paragraphs.

## Draft Contract

- Total duration: provider-specific, with 15 seconds as the current reference default.
- Shot count: 3 to 7 for a 15-second scene.
- Shot duration: 2 to 5 seconds.
- Each shot starts with a timecode.
- Each shot includes shot size, lens shorthand, camera angle, and camera movement.
- Dialogue appears inside the shot where it occurs.
- End with location and audio metadata when the provider prompt benefits from it.
- Stay under the provider's character limit when one exists.

## Variants

- Animation variant: may include dialogue in the fixed audio line and may carry character style from an animation reference sheet.
- Cinematic variant: may include a style metadata line, but that line must come from approved Style Direction rather than a universal default.
- Fashion campaign variant: may use one paragraph, `Cut to.` separators, a 1,000-character limit, movement in every shot, one negative-space wide, and one direct-attitude beat.

## Trim Priority

1. Cut adjectives and atmospheric padding.
2. Merge repeated location details.
3. Shorten repeated character references.
4. Simplify camera specs only after preserving timecodes, dialogue, and shot intent.

## Artist OS Mapping

This is an export shape, not a planning shape. The Video Medium Plan remains the source of truth for shot logic, traceability, and review.
