# Draft Pattern: Provider Video Failure Modes

These failure modes come from early Higgsfield and Seedance-oriented references. They are draft research notes.

## Current Failure Inventory

- Duplicate camera or phone appears in found-footage shots.
- Uploaded audio becomes muffled when delivery over-describes microphone character.
- Locked tripod language freezes the subject as well as the camera.
- Gestures loop instead of evolving through the line.
- Cinematic language causes camera drift when the shot needs a static lock.
- Wide framing weakens face fidelity.
- Walking looks ungrounded unless physical mechanics are named.
- Reference images bleed style, lighting, or subjects beyond their intended role.
- Informal crowd scenes hallucinate podiums, microphones, or stages.
- Warm light collapses to neutral daylight unless reinforced.
- Short clips break when too many action beats are packed into the duration.
- Product logos become illegible unless framing and orientation are locked.
- Fashion exports can lose the garment hierarchy if the model, location, or atmosphere becomes the prompt's subject.
- Streetwear/editorial clips can become static pose slideshows unless movement is required in every shot.
- Provider ad batches cast random presenters when avatar strategy is missing.
- Prompt-only, image, video, and analysis models are misrouted because they expose different media roles.
- Generated campaign rows fail without retry tracking or batch-level permission gates.

## Use

Failure guards should be applied only when relevant to the shot. They belong in provider-target logic, not in core Artist OS theory.
