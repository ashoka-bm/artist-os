# Draft Pattern: Retrieval-First Real-Footage Montage

Status: research draft.

OpenMontage introduces a video path that does not rely on generated motion clips. It builds or searches a footage corpus, selects real clips for planned slots, records provenance, and edits them into a timeline.

## Draft Principle

Real-footage montage should be its own journey. It needs source discovery, license/provenance tracking, slot-level search terms, clip selection, edit logic, music posture, and color/audio normalization.

## Required Planning Elements

- Thematic question or governing idea.
- Tone register.
- Slot list with concrete visual descriptions.
- Search queries per slot.
- Preferred source mix.
- Hero slots and cutaway slots.
- Clip provenance and license.
- Rejected pick notes.
- Edit rhythm and transition vocabulary.
- Music and end-card plan, if needed.

## Known Risks

- Treating stock footage as interchangeable decoration.
- Losing license and source provenance.
- Reusing the same subject or scale across adjacent clips.
- Mixing archive eras without an intentional visual reason.
- Letting music do the emotional work that the footage should carry.

## Artist OS Mapping

This journey can preserve Artist Meaning if the footage slots trace back to Storyboard Shots and Beat roles. It should not be collapsed into generated b-roll prompting.
