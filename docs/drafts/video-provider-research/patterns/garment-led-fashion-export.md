# Draft Pattern: Garment-Led Fashion Export

Status: research draft.

Fashion campaign prompts need a different hierarchy from general character prompts. The garment or styled look is the hero. The model exists to show movement, attitude, fit, silhouette, fabric, and detail.

## Draft Contract

- Keep the export short; current reference limit is 1,000 characters.
- Use one flowing paragraph when the provider prompt needs compression.
- Use 6 to 7 fast shots for a 15-second streetwear/editorial sequence.
- Include model movement in every shot.
- Include at least one negative-space wide.
- Include at least one direct-attitude beat.
- Distribute garment detail across the sequence.
- End with a diegetic audio line when audio is not separately planned.

## Required Fashion Beats

- Negative-space wide: subject small against architecture, sky, void, or ground.
- Attitude lock: direct confident eye contact with a named physical shift.
- Candid mid-motion: garment reacts to body movement.
- Environmental anchor: model moves through or pushes off the space.
- Detail beat: fabric, hardware, seam, logo, footwear, or accessory visible in motion.

## Artist OS Mapping

This is a provider export pattern. Upstream planning should still preserve Artist Meaning, Style Direction, Shot Design, Visual Dynamics, garment reference scope, and any campaign requirements.

## Risks

- Garment detail is lost to model performance.
- Movement is generic and does not reveal fit or fabric.
- Negative space becomes empty instead of editorial.
- The prompt assumes streetwear when the garment belongs to another fashion register.
