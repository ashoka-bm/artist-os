# Draft Journey: Fashion Campaign Video Export

Status: research draft.

## End Product

A compact provider prompt for a high-energy streetwear or editorial fashion campaign clip.

## Likely Inputs

- Garment, outfit, or model reference image.
- Model descriptor.
- Location.
- Action or movement energy.
- Expression or attitude.
- Lighting mood.
- Campaign or brand style direction.

## Prompt Needs

- Garment-first visual analysis.
- Short 15-second edit.
- 6 to 7 fast shots by default.
- Movement in every shot.
- Negative-space wide.
- Direct-attitude beat.
- Garment detail distribution.
- One-paragraph compressed output.

## Known Risks

- The model identity dominates the garment.
- The sequence becomes a pose slideshow.
- No shot clearly shows silhouette.
- The prompt repeats garment description instead of staging new visual information.
- A flat-lay reference lacks model fit data.

## Artist OS Mapping

This journey likely belongs under future fashion/editorial or campaign package planning. It consumes approved reference scope, Style Direction, Visual Dynamics, and Shot Design, then emits provider prompt text. Generated clips require Output Records and review against garment fidelity and campaign intent.
