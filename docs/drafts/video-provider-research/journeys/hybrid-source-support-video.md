# Draft Journey: Hybrid Source-Support Video

Status: research draft.

## End Product

A video that combines source footage with generated or designed support layers: overlays, diagrams, captions, product inserts, screen elements, b-roll, or narration.

## Likely Inputs

- Source footage or screen recording.
- Approved Video Storyboard or brief.
- Source media review.
- Support asset needs.
- Audio posture.
- Target platform and variants.
- Render runtime options.

## Prompt Or Production Needs

- Source-led and support-led beat separation.
- Transcript, scene detection, or frame sampling when source footage exists.
- Overlay density rules.
- Support asset prompts or deterministic render specs.
- Edit decisions that preserve source truth.
- Aspect-ratio variant plan.
- Audio cleanup, captions, and music mix.

## Known Risks

- Generated support material overwhelms the source footage.
- The edit assumes content that the source footage does not contain.
- Overlays reduce readability or emotional force.
- Platform variants crop out important source details.
- Audio from different sources feels mismatched.

## Artist OS Mapping

This journey could extend the Video Storyboard when the artist supplies real footage. The source review should become evidence, and support layers should trace to Beat roles rather than generic decoration.
