# Draft Journey: Sitcom Narrative Sequence

Status: research draft.

## End Product

A short stylized narrative sequence with multiple sitcom scenes, recurring character continuity, repeated comic structure, and genre transitions.

## Likely Inputs

- Creator identity reference image.
- Approved transformed character still or reference sheet.
- Sitcom style target.
- Scene count and chronological order.
- Script or beat outline.
- Setting and roommate or supporting-character requirements.
- Transition convention, such as fade to black.

## Prompt Needs

- Style-transformed identity still before video generation.
- Separate scene prompts or a scene-batched strategy.
- Clear continuity rules for character, wardrobe, set, and era.
- Repeated scene grammar with a planned variation in the final scene.
- Dialogue or reaction beats tied to the AI-replacement reveal.
- Fourth-wall device if the ending needs self-awareness.

## Known Risks

- Character continuity drifts across scenes.
- The provider compresses three scenes into a montage.
- Sitcom style becomes vague nostalgia.
- Dialogue and reveal timing become unclear.
- Supporting characters are under-specified.
- Fourth-wall beat lacks a concrete visual device.

## Artist OS Mapping

This journey starts after a Video Medium Plan exists but may require a calibration still or character reference sheet before provider video prompting. Each sitcom scene should map to Video Scenes and Storyboard Shots. Script drafting should route through Text Journey when exact dialogue matters.
