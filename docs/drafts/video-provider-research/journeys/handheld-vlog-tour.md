# Draft Journey: Handheld Vlog Tour

Status: research draft.

## End Product

A one-take creator-clone vlog where the subject moves through a space and reveals features while speaking to the camera.

## Likely Inputs

- Identity reference image.
- Audio file and exact transcript.
- Setting description or room reference.
- Reveal order, such as bathroom, bed, television, view, or object.
- Duration target.

## Prompt Needs

- Single continuous shot.
- Found-footage or handheld camera logic.
- Clear reveal order tied to transcript beats.
- Decisive environment details.
- Gentle handheld motion rather than aggressive shake.
- Face-fidelity framing at the start or end when the identity must read clearly.

## Known Risks

- Duplicate camera or phone.
- Too many reveals for the duration.
- Handheld shake overwhelms identity.
- Provider invents unrequested room details.
- Dialogue timing drifts if transcript and audio do not match.

## Artist OS Mapping

This journey consumes a storyboard-ready sequence with one scene, one continuous shot, dialogue-led audio posture, and blocking that maps speech beats to spatial reveals.
