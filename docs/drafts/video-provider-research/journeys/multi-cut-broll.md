# Draft Journey: Multi-Cut B-Roll

Status: research draft.

## End Product

A short sequence of deliberate cuts that shows action, mood, setup, packing, mirror work, product handling, or travel movement.

## Likely Inputs

- Identity or subject reference.
- Optional prop references.
- Setting and lighting direction.
- Cut count and duration.
- Action list or storyboard panels.

## Prompt Needs

- Multi-cut shot family.
- One timed action block per cut.
- Explicit cut timestamps.
- Continuity rules for wardrobe, lighting, props, and setting.
- Same-angle jump-cut logic when the camera should not move between cuts.

## Known Risks

- Unintended camera movement.
- Lighting changes between cuts.
- Reference props mutate.
- Cuts feel rushed when action density is too high.
- Same-angle montage becomes a new camera angle per cut unless locked.

## Artist OS Mapping

This journey consumes multiple storyboard shots or one scene with timed cut blocks. It should preserve Beat Plan movement while using provider-specific timing syntax only at export.
