# Draft Journey: Compact Multi-Shot Cinematic Export

Status: research draft.

## End Product

A short ready-to-paste cinematic provider prompt built from a reference image and storyboard-ready scene.

## Likely Inputs

- Reference image.
- Approved Video Medium Plan or scene brief.
- Action.
- Location and time of day.
- Style Direction.
- Audio posture.
- Provider character limit.

## Prompt Needs

- Compact subject description.
- Timecoded shot paragraphs.
- Shot size, lens, camera angle, and camera movement per shot.
- Location metadata.
- Style metadata derived from approved Style Direction.
- Audio metadata derived from Audio Plan.
- Character-limit trimming.

## Known Risks

- The export hardcodes a style that conflicts with the approved brief.
- Camera grammar crowds out emotional intent.
- The strict duration does not fit the planned scene.
- The prompt over-describes the reference image.
- Multiple characters lack enough introduction space.

## Artist OS Mapping

This journey sits after storyboard approval. It should render provider prompt text from the Video Medium Plan without changing the plan. The style line must come from approved Style Direction rather than a universal default.
