# Draft Journey: Animated Character Sequence

Status: research draft.

## End Product

A short animated clip featuring a recurring character with consistent design, style, action, and camera movement.

## Likely Inputs

- Character reference sheet.
- Target animation style.
- Setting or environment reference.
- One action or short beat sequence.
- Camera movement.
- Optional second character, prop, or obstacle reference.

## Prompt Needs

- Character sheet or approved character design.
- Style Direction or style calibration.
- Clear action path.
- Camera behavior.
- Continuity lock for character design.
- Provider reference-scope rules.

## Known Risks

- Character design drifts during motion.
- The clip becomes a montage when a one-take is desired.
- Camera movement dominates the action.
- Animated style becomes generic without a calibrated reference.
- Secondary references bleed into the main character.

## Artist OS Mapping

This journey consumes a storyboard-ready Video Medium Plan and may require pre-generation of reference sheets or calibration stills. Generated clips need Output Records linked to Storyboard Shots.
