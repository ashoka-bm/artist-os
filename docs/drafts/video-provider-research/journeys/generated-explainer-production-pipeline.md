# Draft Journey: Generated Explainer Production Pipeline

Status: research draft.

## End Product

A complete explainer video with research, script, narration or captions, generated or designed visuals, music, edit decisions, and a final render.

## Likely Inputs

- Topic or approved Video Creative Brief.
- Target audience.
- Target duration and platform.
- Video Storyboard or scene plan.
- Style Direction or playbook.
- Audio posture.
- Available providers and render runtimes.
- Budget limit.

## Prompt Or Production Needs

- Research brief with cited facts and audience questions.
- Proposal with concept options and cost paths.
- Script with narration timing and visual enhancement cues.
- Scene plan with required assets.
- Asset manifest with provider metadata and provenance.
- Edit decisions with subtitles, music ducking, and transitions.
- Render validation.

## Known Risks

- Research becomes generic or unsupported.
- Concept approval happens after assets are generated.
- Visuals become animated cards without enough motion logic.
- Narration, music, and captions are deferred until they fail late.
- Render runtime is chosen too late to guide visual design.

## Artist OS Mapping

Artist OS already owns meaning, beat, style, and storyboard planning. This journey would only start after storyboard approval and should convert the approved plan into an execution package.
