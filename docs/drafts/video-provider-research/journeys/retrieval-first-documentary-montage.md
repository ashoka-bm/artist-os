# Draft Journey: Retrieval-First Documentary Montage

Status: research draft.

## End Product

A thematic montage assembled from real footage found in open archives, stock libraries, or a local footage corpus.

## Likely Inputs

- Approved Video Storyboard or thematic brief.
- Governing question or idea.
- Tone register.
- Target duration and platform.
- Slot list with visual descriptions.
- Preferred footage sources.
- Music posture.
- License/provenance requirements.

## Prompt Or Production Needs

- Search queries for each footage slot.
- Clip selection criteria.
- Hero/cutaway role assignment.
- Edit rhythm and hold duration.
- Transition vocabulary.
- Color-grade strategy across mixed sources.
- Music sync or silence rationale.
- Source provenance and rejected-pick notes.

## Known Risks

- Decorative stock footage weakens Artist Meaning.
- Clip sources or licenses are not recorded.
- Adjacent shots repeat subject, scale, or motion.
- Archive footage changes the tone unintentionally.
- The edit uses a strong style reference too literally.

## Artist OS Mapping

This is a downstream production route after storyboard approval. It should translate Storyboard Shots into retrieval slots and timeline decisions while preserving Beat roles and provenance.
