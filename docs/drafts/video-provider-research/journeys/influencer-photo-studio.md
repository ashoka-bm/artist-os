# Draft Journey: Influencer Photo Studio

Status: research draft.

## End Product

A set of still images that place an approved influencer identity into controlled locations, poses, wardrobes, and brand contexts.

## Likely Inputs

- Canonical identity image.
- Character sheet or wardrobe reference.
- Location.
- Time of day.
- Pose or stance.
- Vibe.
- Aspect ratio.
- Optional product or prop references.

## Prompt Needs

- Treat the task as a reference edit when identity images are present.
- Keep the prompt short and directive.
- Tell the provider what to change: pose, placement, scene, lighting, framing, expression, and prop behavior.
- Avoid re-describing the identity reference.
- Scope wardrobe and product references explicitly.

## Known Risks

- Standing pose from reference overrides requested sitting pose.
- Product or prop is duplicated or worn instead of held.
- Outfit and held product references get conflated.
- Background people appear in intimate or controlled scenes.
- Long prompt detail dilutes the actual edit request.

## Artist OS Mapping

This journey can support calibration stills, campaign stills, or visual reference expansion. Generated images require Output Records and should link back to the identity kit and any relevant Creative Brief or package plan.
