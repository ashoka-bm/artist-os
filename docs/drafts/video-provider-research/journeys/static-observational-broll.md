# Draft Journey: Static Observational B-Roll

Status: research draft.

## End Product

A short locked-camera shot where the subject or scene moves while the camera remains fixed.

## Likely Inputs

- Identity or subject reference image.
- Optional prop reference.
- Setting and time of day.
- Entry and exit direction.
- Duration target.

## Prompt Needs

- Single continuous shot.
- Static observational style when camera movement would break the intent.
- Strong composition lock.
- Explicit subject movement through the frame.
- Grounded physical motion for walking or prop handling.

## Known Risks

- Cinematic wording causes camera drift.
- Subject movement looks ungrounded.
- Wide framing weakens face detail.
- Lighting changes across the shot.

## Artist OS Mapping

This journey consumes a storyboard shot with static camera movement, subject movement as the active event, and Shot Design that prioritizes body action, environment, or scale.
