# Draft Journey: Creator Clone Talking Head

Status: research draft.

## End Product

A short, dialogue-led clip where an identity reference appears to speak an uploaded audio line in a controlled talking-head setup.

## Likely Inputs

- Identity reference image.
- Audio file for lip-sync.
- Exact transcript.
- Setting or existing frame reference.
- Duration from audio length.
- Optional microphone or desk context.

## Prompt Needs

- Single continuous shot.
- Medium close-up or close framing when face fidelity matters.
- Exact dialogue preserved.
- Minimal delivery line tied to uploaded audio.
- Camera lock that does not freeze natural head movement or gestures.
- Short closing beat if audio ends before the clip does.

## Known Risks

- Frozen subject.
- Looping gesture.
- Muffled audio from over-described delivery.
- Plastic face if framing is too wide.
- Transcript too long for duration.

## Artist OS Mapping

This journey consumes a storyboard-ready shot with dialogue-led `audio_plan`, exact `text_track_refs`, and Shot Design emphasizing face reaction.
