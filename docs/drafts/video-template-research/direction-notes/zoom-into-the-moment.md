# Direction Notes: Zoom Into The Moment

Status: research draft.

Use this when a story beat needs to become a scene instead of a summary.

## Five Moment Anchors

- **Location:** where is the subject physically?
- **Action:** what is happening right now?
- **Thought:** what raw thought is running through the subject's mind?
- **Visible emotion:** what does the feeling look like on the body, face, or behavior?
- **Dialogue:** what exact words are spoken?

## Placement In The Flow

- Beat Plan identifies the beat and its emotional turn.
- Video Medium Plan decides whether the beat should be staged as a scene, narrated, shown as b-roll, or rendered as graphics.
- Script drafting uses the moment anchors when a beat needs immediacy.
- Storyboard Shots translate the moment into location, blocking, gesture, expression, and sound.

## Checks

- Are we inside a specific moment, or summarizing from above?
- Can the viewer picture where the scene is?
- Does the action create forward motion?
- Does the thought sound human rather than polished?
- Is the emotion visible?
- Does dialogue reveal the turn faster than explanation?
