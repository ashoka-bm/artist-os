# Direction Notes: Narrative Depth Routing

Status: research draft.

Narrative Depth decides how much story structure a video output needs.

## Levels

| Level | Needs | Example Outputs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `full_story` | Story Template | personal story, mini documentary, reframe explainer, cinematic sketch |
| `micro_journey` | Micro-Journey Template | unboxing, product reveal, influencer clip, UGC testimonial, fashion fit check |
| `utility_sequence` | purpose, role, constraints, success criteria | b-roll loop, title card, product spin, style test, transition plate |

## Routing Rule

Ask what the output must do for the viewer:

- If the viewer must experience a change in understanding, feeling, belief, or identification, use `full_story`.
- If the viewer must notice, trust, desire, understand, or remember one thing, use `micro_journey`.
- If the clip supports another artifact and is not meant to stand alone, use `utility_sequence`.

## Minimum Requirements

### Full Story

- opening tension or hook,
- desire or question,
- obstacle or conflict,
- turn or change,
- payoff or result.

### Micro-Journey

- hook,
- object of attention,
- reason to care,
- proof, reveal, or sensory payoff,
- ending beat.

### Utility Sequence

- role in larger video,
- subject,
- visual purpose,
- duration,
- motion,
- style constraints,
- success criteria.
