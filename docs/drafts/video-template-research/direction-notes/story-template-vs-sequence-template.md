# Direction Notes: Story Template vs Sequence Template

Status: research draft.

## Story Template

A story template defines the audience journey. It needs a hook, turn, payoff, and reason to keep watching.

Examples:

- We saw this -> people think it means this -> it actually means this -> here is the move.
- Problem -> failed fix -> better move -> proof.
- Myth -> reality -> practical rule.

## Sequence Template

A sequence template defines shot order, motion, or provider packaging. It can make a scene easier to generate, but it does not guarantee a story.

Examples:

- Wide -> medium -> close-up.
- 3-7 timecoded shots.
- One-take talking head.
- Start-frame -> end-frame animation.
- Last frame -> next clip continuity chain.

## Working Rule

Use sequence templates to express story templates. Do not let sequence templates replace story structure.
