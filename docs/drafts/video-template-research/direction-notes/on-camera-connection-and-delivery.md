# Direction Notes: On-Camera Connection And Delivery

Status: research draft.

These notes apply to creator-led videos, talking-head formats, educational videos, explainers, UGC, and any format where a person speaks directly or semi-directly to the viewer.

## Principles

- Speak like one person talking to one person.
- Prefer connection over polish.
- Know the Intended Feeling before scripting or recording.
- Support every major point with "paint": example, analogy, metaphor, story, demonstration, or concrete scene.
- Warm up voice, body, and purpose before recording.

## Flow Placement

- Artist Meaning captures what must survive and the Intended Feeling.
- Beat Plan owns the story movement.
- Video Medium Plan should add performance posture when the selected format is speaker-led.
- Script drafting should apply the conversational voice and point-plus-paint checks.
- Provider Export should preserve delivery posture when using voiceover, dialogue, avatar, or creator clone workflows.

## Checks

- Would this line sound natural to a friend across a table?
- Does the speaker sound like they are connecting or proving themselves?
- Does the video know how the viewer should feel?
- Does each key point have an example or image the viewer can hold?
- Does the delivery plan include energy, pace, and relationship to the viewer?
