# Story Template: Misread Signal -> Reframe -> Move

Status: research draft.

## Core Pattern

The audience sees a signal and inherits the obvious interpretation. The video reveals that the obvious interpretation is incomplete or wrong, then gives the viewer a more useful move.

## Structure

1. **Signal:** "We ran into this."
2. **Common read:** "Most people think it means this."
3. **Reframe:** "It actually means this."
4. **Implication:** "That changes the decision."
5. **Move:** "Here is what to do next."
6. **Payoff:** The viewer leaves with sharper judgment, not only information.

## Required Inputs

- The signal, event, mistake, comment, metric, object, trend, or scene.
- The common interpretation.
- The truer interpretation.
- The consequence of misreading it.
- The recommended move.
- Proof, example, or demonstration.

## Best Format Fits

- Talking head with supporting b-roll.
- Motion graphics explainer.
- Documentary montage.
- Hybrid source-support video.
- Compact cinematic scene if the signal can be dramatized visually.

## Hook And Payoff

The hook should name the signal and the mistaken read quickly. The payoff should give the viewer a decision rule they can use later.

## Seedance 2 Fit

Seedance can render one beat of this template at a time. Do not force the whole template into one 15-second clip unless the idea is extremely simple.
