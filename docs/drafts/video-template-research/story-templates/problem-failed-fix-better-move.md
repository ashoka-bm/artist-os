# Story Template: Problem -> Failed Fix -> Better Move

Status: research draft.

## Core Pattern

The audience recognizes a problem. The video shows why the obvious fix does not work, then offers a more useful move.

## Structure

1. **Problem:** Name the pain or friction.
2. **Obvious fix:** Show the common response.
3. **Failure:** Explain or demonstrate why it breaks.
4. **Better move:** Show the more effective action.
5. **Proof:** Give example, result, or comparison.
6. **Payoff:** The viewer knows what to change.

## Required Inputs

- The problem.
- The common fix.
- The failure mode.
- The better move.
- A concrete proof point.

## Best Format Fits

- Talking head with b-roll.
- Screen demo.
- Product UGC.
- Motion graphics explainer.
- Hybrid source-support video.
