# Story Template: Myth -> Reality -> Move

Status: research draft.

## Core Pattern

The video starts with a belief the audience recognizes, breaks it with a concrete contradiction, then gives a better rule.

## Structure

1. **Myth:** Name the belief.
2. **Why it feels true:** Show the surface evidence.
3. **Reality:** Reveal what is actually happening.
4. **Cost:** Show what the myth causes people to do wrong.
5. **Move:** Give the better rule or action.
6. **Payoff:** The audience can now spot the trap.

## Required Inputs

- The myth.
- The reason people believe it.
- The counter-evidence.
- The better explanation.
- The recommended move.

## Best Format Fits

- Talking head.
- Motion graphics explainer.
- UGC/product explainer.
- Hybrid source-support video.
- Documentary montage.
