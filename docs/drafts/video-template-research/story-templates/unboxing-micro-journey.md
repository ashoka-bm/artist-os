# Micro-Journey Template: Unboxing Reveal

Status: research draft.

## Core Pattern

An unboxing is usually not a full story. It is a micro-journey from curiosity to reveal to proof.

## Structure

1. **Curiosity:** What is inside, or why should the viewer care?
2. **First reveal:** The box opens and the product appears.
3. **Detail proof:** Texture, feature, scale, packaging, reaction, or use case.
4. **Payoff:** Why the product matters, how it feels, or what changes after seeing it.
5. **Exit beat:** final product pose, reaction, CTA, or next action.

## Required Inputs

- Product or object.
- Viewer reason to care.
- Reveal order.
- Detail proof.
- Desired feeling.
- Exit beat.

## Best Format Fits

- UGC product video.
- Influencer clip.
- Product reveal.
- Short social post.
- Multi-cut b-roll.

## Artist OS Mapping

This is a Micro-Journey Template, not a full Story Template. It should be used when the product reveal needs hook and payoff but not a complete situation/desire/conflict/change/result arc.
