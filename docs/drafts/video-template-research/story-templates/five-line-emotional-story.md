# Story Template: Five-Line Emotional Story

Status: research draft.

## Core Pattern

A story becomes clear when it can be reduced to five lines: situation, desire, conflict, change, and result. The five lines are not the script. They are the structural spine.

## Structure

1. **Situation:** Where are we, who is involved, and what is normal at the start?
2. **Desire:** What does the character, creator, audience, or subject want?
3. **Conflict:** What blocks that desire or creates tension?
4. **Change:** What shifts through insight, action, decision, or event?
5. **Result:** What is different at the end?

## Required Inputs

- Starting situation.
- Main subject or character.
- Desire or need.
- Obstacle or tension.
- Turning point.
- Ending state.

## Best Format Fits

- Personal story video.
- Creator lesson video.
- Mini documentary.
- Product founder story.
- Cinematic sketch.
- Animated short.
- Hybrid source-support video.

## Hook And Payoff

The hook should surface either the conflict or the promise of change. The payoff should make the result feel different from the situation.

## Artist OS Mapping

This is a Story Template candidate. It belongs before Video Medium Plan. The Video Medium Plan can then choose whether the story becomes a talking head, cinematic sketch, montage, animation, or other format.

## Seedance 2 Fit

For Seedance 2, the five-line story usually needs multiple 15-second-or-less clips. One clip can carry one line, one turn, or one compact scene.
