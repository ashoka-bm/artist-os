# Research-Backed Utility Sequence Template Library

Status: research draft.

Use these when `narrative_depth = utility_sequence`. Utility sequences need purpose, traceability, Intended Feeling, and payoff, but they should not invent conflict, character arc, or story turns.

The Video Medium Plan should carry the selected utility intent through `asset_purpose_brief`.

## Starting Set

| Template | Use Case | Functional Purpose | Expected Parts | Motion Behavior | Loop Or Resolution | Common Failure Mode |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `utility_process_steps` | Tutorials, recipes, maker/process footage | Make an action legible. | Establish context, ordered step units, result/check. | Wide/medium/close progression, hands/details, continuity. | Resolves on completed state. | Pretty but unclear coverage. |
| `utility_broll_coverage_set` | Interviews, essays, vlogs, documentaries | Cover cuts, add context, and align visuals to spoken meaning. | Establishing, cutaway, insert, atmosphere, reaction/detail. | Static shots, pans, reveals, push-ins. | Modular non-looping clips. | Random wallpaper shots. |
| `utility_product_demo_walkthrough` | SaaS, product walkthroughs, sales/support clips | Show what a product does and why it matters. | User problem, feature action, benefit/result, CTA or next step. | Cursor focus, zooms, highlights, controlled pans. | Resolves on completed task. | Feature tour with no user need. |
| `utility_explainer_diagram_insert` | Education, marketing, technical explanation | Turn abstract logic into visual structure. | Title/context, nodes, reveal order, summary/payoff. | Staged reveals, arrows, callouts, simple transforms. | Resolves to full diagram or hold frame. | Too many labels or no hierarchy. |
| `utility_callout_annotation_pack` | Product shots, screen recordings, reviews, explainers | Direct attention to one exact detail. | Focus marker, connector/arrow, label, optional dim/blur. | Enter, track or hold, exit. | Timed overlay. | Occludes the subject or tracks poorly. |
| `utility_graphics_identity_package` | Channels, courses, broadcasts, release packages | Create a reusable branded visual system. | Opener, logo sting, lower third, bug, bumper, end card. | Shared timing, easing, choreography. | Mix of resolving stings and persistent overlays. | Inconsistent animation grammar. |
| `utility_title_chapter_cards` | Section breaks, lessons, episodes, portfolios | Orient the viewer and reset attention. | Main title, chapter card, intertitle, optional credits. | Simple reveal, hold, clear. | Resolves into next section. | Overlong cards or poor mobile readability. |
| `utility_transition_bridge_set` | Scene changes, topic shifts, event packages | Connect or separate parts cleanly. | Cut rule, wipe/stinger, sound bridge, optional bumper. | Match cut, wipe, morph, push, hard cut, or bridge. | Resolves from A to B. | Distracting transition effect. |
| `utility_looping_background_or_hold` | Live screens, waiting rooms, ambient posts, title backgrounds | Sustain atmosphere without demanding narrative attention. | Base motif, subtle motion layer, texture, safe text zones. | Cyclic, low-distraction motion. | Seamless loop or optional resolved version. | Visible seam or too much motion behind text. |

## Asset Purpose Brief Requirements

For any utility sequence, `asset_purpose_brief` should capture:

- role or use context;
- subject and visual purpose;
- placement;
- duration target;
- shot or asset count;
- motion behavior;
- loop or resolution behavior;
- style constraints;
- reference or continuity needs;
- audio/text posture;
- success criteria;
- downstream export notes.

## Review Checks To Add Later

- Is the asset useful in its stated placement?
- Does each shot or graphic serve a clear job?
- Is the package coherent across typography, motion, color, and pacing?
- Does the sequence avoid fake story language?
- Is the payoff functional: clarity, coverage, orientation, transition, proof, loop, or reuse?
