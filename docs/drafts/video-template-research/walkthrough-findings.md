## What The Walkthroughs Proved

The three-way routing is useful:

- `full_story` fits videos with desire, escalation, reversal, and payoff.
- `micro_journey` fits compact creator/product/social videos with hook-to-payoff movement but no full Story Structure.
- `utility_sequence` fits B-roll, motion graphics, and asset packages that need purpose and success criteria rather than story turns.

Provider preference notes worked as downstream notes. None of the walkthroughs needed Seedance, Higgsfield, or another provider to decide the story layer, format layer, or storyboard scope.

## Full Story Findings

Test packet: 90s sitcom AI-replacement sketch.

Fit: `full_story`.

Why it worked:

- The repeated credential scenes create escalation, not just sequence.
- The final fourth-wall beat creates a turn and payoff.
- `five_line_emotional_story` worked as the primary story movement.
- `problem_failed_fix_better_move` worked as a secondary pattern.

New pattern candidate:

- repeated-escalation sketch: a character repeats the same attempted solution across escalating situations until the repeated structure reveals the real problem.

Risks before promotion:

- The final Artist Meaning must be confirmed: AI anxiety, reinvention, or durable creative judgment.
- Dialogue belongs in Text Journey; Video Medium Plan should carry refs and timing.
- Provider duration limits may split the piece into multiple clips, but that is a Production Route issue.

## Micro-Journey Findings

Test packet: AI video workflow toolkit reveal.

Fit: `micro_journey`, selected template `product_reveal`.

Why it worked:

- The toolkit is the object of attention.
- The viewer reason to care is repeatable B-roll from an approved still.
- The proof path is still frame -> workflow components -> animated B-roll package.
- A five-shot storyboard scope was enough for the draft walkthrough.

Chooser improvement:

- Add overlap guidance for `product_reveal` vs `unboxing_reveal` and `product_reveal` vs `creator_showcase_moment`.

Risks before promotion:

- `micro_journey_template_ref` is durable now, and the enum supports the first nine promoted micro-journey ids.
- More human-led tests are needed, especially `ugc_testimonial` and `creator_showcase_moment`.
- Video Critic Review may eventually need a micro-journey check: object of attention, viewer reason to care, proof/payoff, and ending beat.

## Candidate Micro-Journey Findings

The candidate micro-journey walkthroughs showed that some short videos needed more precise compact journeys than the initial seven schema-supported ids provided. `problem_solution_demo` and `how_to_tip_demo` have now moved from candidate to promoted schema-supported ids; `social_proof_receipt` remains draft-only until it has one more proof type test.

### `problem_solution_demo`

Test packet: 20-30 second short social video for an AI video workflow tool.

Fit: `micro_journey`.

Current schema-supported fallback: `quick_before_after_demo` or `product_reveal`.

Why it matters:

- The viewer movement is friction -> solution entry -> visible proof -> relief.
- The product is not only revealed; it earns trust by solving a named problem.
- The proof is broader than visible before/after because causal relief matters.

Second evidence packet: kitchen scraper short.

Updated recommendation: **promoted `problem_solution_demo`**. The second walkthrough confirmed the shape works outside software.

### `how_to_tip_demo`

Test packet: 20-30 second Reel or Short teaching one repeatable move for turning an approved still into an animation-ready prompt or storyboard shot.

Fit: `micro_journey`.

Current schema-supported fallback: `creator_showcase_moment`.

Why it matters:

- The viewer movement is confusion -> promised tip -> compressed steps -> visible result -> repeat/use cue.
- `utility_process_steps` can support the middle, but it does not govern the hook, promise, proof, or payoff.
- The creator can appear, but the memory should be the repeatable action.

Second evidence packet: talking-head lighting tip.

Updated recommendation: **promoted `how_to_tip_demo`**. The second walkthrough confirmed the shape works outside AI-video prompting.

### `social_proof_receipt`

Test packet: 15-25 second short built around comments, DMs, reviews, or request receipts that create demand before the product or tutorial appears.

Fit: `micro_journey`.

Current schema-supported fallback: `ugc_testimonial`.

Why it matters:

- The proof artifact carries trust rather than a speaker's lived experience.
- It needs provenance, privacy, consent, legibility, and claim-safety handling.
- It may become its own template, but it is still close to testimonial logic.

Second evidence packet: waitlist demand release short.

Updated recommendation: **defer promotion, keep candidate active**. The second walkthrough strengthens the case for a distinct proof-artifact-led template, but one more case should test review/rating proof before adding a schema enum value.

## Utility Sequence Findings

Test packet: The Explainer Video Toolkit motion graphics package.

Fit: `utility_sequence`.

Why it worked:

- The seven graphics are functional assets, not story beats.
- Asset Purpose Brief gave enough structure without fake conflict.
- The package needed readability, coherence, and functional payoff more than narrative arc.

Possible future output shapes:

- `motion_graphics_package`
- `b_roll_package`

Risks before promotion:

- `utility_sequence` and `asset_purpose_brief` are durable now, but utility-specific reviewer checks still need testing.
- Existing `storyboard_shots` require Beat and Key Emotional Movement refs, which may be heavy for pure utility assets.
- Utility-specific review checks may be needed: readability, package coherence, motion usefulness, functional payoff, and avoidance of fake story.

## Cultural Format Findings

### `documentary_mini_profile`

Test packet: 60-90 second mini-profile of a creator whose real value is taste, judgment, emotional direction, and decision-making rather than tool operation.

Fit: Cultural Format Structure candidate.

Recommended `narrative_depth`: `micro_journey` by default, with `full_story` available when the profile includes a larger life or career turn.

Current route: route as `micro_journey`, usually with `creator_showcase_moment` when the creator's judgment is the compact memory, and record `documentary_mini_profile` as the Cultural Format Structure.

Why it matters:

- The missing shape is not a Story Template; it is an audience-facing format grammar.
- The core parts are human entry, observed work, context / nut graf, tension, proof, return, and kicker.
- Without a named format, agents may misclassify profile work as explainers, creator showcases, UGC testimonials, or mood montages.

Second evidence packet: ceramicist mini-profile.

Updated recommendation: **promoted `documentary_mini_profile` as a Cultural Format Structure**. The second walkthrough confirmed the shape works outside AI-creator content.
