## What It Is

This transcript explains why creator videos lose retention when the speaker sounds formal, performs for judgment, lacks a clear intended feeling, states unsupported points, or starts recording cold.

It is not a story template. It is direction for script voice, audience connection, emotional posture, and on-camera performance.

## Reusable Notes

- **Conversational voice:** if a line would sound strange across a cafe table, it will probably sound strange on camera.
- **Connection over perfection:** the speaker should aim to connect with one viewer, not perform for an imagined crowd.
- **Intended Feeling:** the creator must know how the viewer should feel before recording or scripting.
- **Point plus paint:** every important point needs an example, analogy, metaphor, story, or demonstration.
- **Warm start:** delivery improves when the speaker warms body, voice, and purpose before recording.

## Artist OS Mapping

These notes belong in direction guidance for talking-head, explainer, education, and creator-led formats.

The Intended Feeling point already matches Artist OS language. It should be captured upstream during Artist Meaning and Creative Brief work, then carried into script, Video Audio Posture, and performance direction.

## Draft Rule

Creator-led video scripts should not only state what the video says. They should define how the speaker should relate to the viewer, how the viewer should feel, and what concrete "paint" makes each point land.

## Risks

- The script is structurally correct but sounds like a presentation.
- The creator optimizes wording but loses connection.
- Emotional posture is unclear, so the viewer cannot tell whether the video is meant to inspire, teach, confess, warn, or sell.
- Points stack up without examples or concrete scenes.
- Performance direction appears too late, after the storyboard and script have already hardened.
