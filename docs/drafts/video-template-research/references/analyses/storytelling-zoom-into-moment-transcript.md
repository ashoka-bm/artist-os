## What It Is

This transcript explains how to make a story feel immediate instead of summarized. The central rule is to zoom into the moment rather than describe the event from far away.

It is not a full Story Template by itself. It is a direction layer for turning a story beat into a vivid scene.

## Reusable Notes

- **Location:** name where the moment physically happens so the viewer can picture it.
- **Action:** state what the subject is doing in the moment.
- **Thought:** include raw, human thought rather than polished explanation.
- **Emotion:** show feeling through body, face, pace, or gesture instead of only naming it.
- **Dialogue:** use exact, concise words when another person speaks.

## Artist OS Mapping

This belongs in direction notes for script drafting, scene design, and Storyboard Shot planning.

It should activate when a Story Template or Micro-Journey Template requires a lived moment, anecdote, reveal, confrontation, or demonstration.

## Draft Rule

When a Beat needs to become a scene, check whether it has location, action, thought, visible emotion, and dialogue or sound. Not every scene needs all five, but weak story scenes often lack most of them.

## Risks

- The system summarizes the story instead of staging the moment.
- The script explains emotions instead of making them visible.
- Dialogue sounds formal rather than human.
- Too many location details slow the hook.
- Raw thought becomes over-written and loses immediacy.
