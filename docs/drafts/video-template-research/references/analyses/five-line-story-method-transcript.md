## What It Is

This transcript presents a simple story skeleton that can scale from a five-line outline to a longer video. Its value is that it protects the emotional core before shot lists, transitions, gear, provider prompts, or editing details distract the creator.

## Core Structure

1. **Situation:** where we are, who is involved, and what the world looks like.
2. **Desire:** what the character wants or needs.
3. **Conflict:** what gets in the way.
4. **Change:** what shifts through insight, action, or turning point.
5. **Result:** how the ending differs from the beginning.

## Artist OS Mapping

This belongs near Story Structure. It can inform the Beat Plan before Video Medium Plan selects format and shot structure.

The five-line structure can also act as a check on video templates: every format should preserve situation, desire, conflict, change, and result unless the artist intentionally chooses a fragment or mood piece.

## Draft Rule

Use the five-line structure as a story-core test. If a proposed short video cannot answer these five lines, it may still be a useful sequence, montage, or b-roll package, but it is not yet a story template.

## Risks

- Treating the five lines as a full script instead of a structural spine.
- Forcing desire/conflict onto videos that are intentionally observational or poetic.
- Letting format choices hide a weak story core.
- Expanding details before the change and result are clear.
