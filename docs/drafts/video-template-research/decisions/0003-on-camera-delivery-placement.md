# Draft Decision 0003: On-Camera Delivery Placement

Status: accepted in draft.

Date: 2026-06-26

## Decision

On-camera delivery direction is split across the flow:

- **Intended Feeling** is captured upstream in Artist Meaning, Transformation Brief, and Creative Brief work.
- **Speaker posture** is decided in the Video Medium Plan when the selected format is speaker-led.
- **Conversational voice** and **point-plus-paint support** are enforced during script drafting.
- Provider Export preserves the approved delivery posture when rendering voiceover, dialogue, avatar, creator clone, or talking-head prompts.

## Rationale

Delivery direction depends on story meaning and viewer feeling, so it cannot be invented late at prompt export. But it also should not clutter every Beat Plan, because not every video has a speaker.

The Video Medium Plan is the right place to decide whether the speaker should feel like a friend across a table, a teacher, a guide, a witness, a performer, a founder, or a character.

## Consequences

- Speaker-led formats need a delivery posture field or section in their draft format template.
- Scripts should be checked for conversational voice.
- Key points should include examples, analogies, stories, demonstrations, or concrete images.
- Provider-specific prompts should not overwrite the approved delivery posture with generic "confident presenter" language.
