# Draft Decision 0004: Hook Posture Placement

Status: accepted in draft.

Date: 2026-06-26

## Decision

Hook posture is split across the flow:

- The Beat Plan defines the turn, tension, misconception, desire, or promise the hook must open.
- The Video Medium Plan chooses the hook posture once the format is known.
- Script drafting writes the exact hook.
- Provider Export renders the hook safely for the chosen platform.

## Hook Postures

- Question.
- Surprising statement.
- Story-in-the-moment.
- Big promise.
- Visual action.

## Rationale

Hooks need story authority and format awareness. If they are chosen before the Beat Plan, they may create attention without serving the payoff. If they are chosen only at Provider Export, they arrive too late and can distort the storyboard or script.

## Consequences

- Beat Plan should expose the opening tension or turn clearly enough for later hook selection.
- Video Medium Plan should include a hook posture for short-form, creator-led, explainer, UGC, educational, and social-video formats.
- Script drafting should check that the hook points to the payoff.
- Provider-specific exports should not replace an approved hook with generic attention bait.
