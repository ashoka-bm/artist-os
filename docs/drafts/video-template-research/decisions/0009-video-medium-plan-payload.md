# Draft Decision 0009: Video Medium Plan Payload

Status: accepted in draft.

Date: 2026-06-26

## Decision

Video Medium Plan should carry the decisions downstream script, storyboard, and provider export need so they do not improvise.

At minimum, it should carry:

- Narrative Depth.
- Binding Format Template.
- Selected Story Template or Micro-Journey Template, if any.
- Hook posture.
- Speaker posture, when speaker-led.
- Video Audio Posture.
- Video Style Expression.
- Reference strategy.
- Storyboard scope.
- Provider preferences as non-binding notes.

Provider-specific settings stay out of Video Medium Plan until Production Route.

## Rationale

Video Medium Plan is the authority for video-specific translation. It should be complete enough to guide script, storyboard, shot design, and post-storyboard export, while still preserving provider neutrality.

## Consequences

- Script and storyboard work can follow one binding video plan.
- Provider export cannot invent hook posture, speaker posture, format, narrative depth, or reference strategy.
- Provider preferences remain visible without becoming provider lock-in.
- Production Route remains the boundary for Seedance 2, Higgsfield, OpenMontage, Remotion, HyperFrames, FFmpeg, and related platform settings.
