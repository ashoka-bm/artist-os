# Draft Decision 0011: Micro-Journey Template Placement

Status: accepted in draft.

Date: 2026-06-26

## Decision

Micro-Journey Templates stay adjacent to Story Templates in the draft library, but they are lower-depth structures selected through Narrative Depth.

They should not enter the main Story Structure Library as full story structures unless they include desire, conflict, turn, and result.

In implementation, they may become a `micro_journey` subset of Cultural Format Structure or a Video Template Library rather than Story Structure.

## Rationale

Micro-journeys need more than a sequence, but less than a full story. Product reveals, unboxings, fashion fit checks, UGC clips, and influencer moments need hook, object of attention, reason to care, proof/reveal/payoff, and ending beat. They usually do not need a complete story arc.

## Consequences

- Narrative Depth selects Micro-Journey Template when the output is functional, social, promotional, or showcase-led.
- Micro-Journey Templates can sit near Story Templates during research for discoverability.
- Review should reject micro-journeys that have no payoff.
- Review should also reject attempts to force full Story Template machinery onto simple showcase clips.
