# Draft Decision 0005: Production Route After Storyboard

Status: accepted in draft.

Date: 2026-06-26

## Decision

Provider or production route is not chosen as a binding decision before the storyboard exists.

Artist OS may capture a provider preference early if the artist names one, but the binding **Production Route** is selected only after Video Medium Plan and storyboard approval.

## Rationale

Provider choice depends on the actual video plan: format, references, duration, audio posture, style, shot complexity, subject count, continuity needs, budget, and platform limits. Choosing the provider too early lets platform convenience shape the story and storyboard.

## Consequences

- Early provider preference is a non-binding preference, like early Format Intent.
- Video Medium Plan stays provider-neutral.
- Storyboard approval becomes the handoff point into Production Route.
- Production Route can choose Seedance 2, Higgsfield, OpenMontage, Remotion, HyperFrames, FFmpeg, or another route based on the approved storyboard.
- Provider Export should not revise Artist Meaning, Beat Plan, Story Template, Format Template, or approved Storyboard Shots.
