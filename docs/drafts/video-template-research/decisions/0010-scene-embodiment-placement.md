# Draft Decision 0010: Scene Embodiment Placement

Status: accepted in draft.

Date: 2026-06-26

## Decision

Scene-embodiment direction stays as Direction Notes.

The placement is:

- Beat Plan defines the emotional turn.
- Video Medium Plan decides whether the Beat becomes a scene, narration, b-roll, graphics, or another video treatment.
- Script and storyboard drafting apply moment anchors when immediacy is needed.

## Moment Anchors

- Location.
- Action.
- Raw thought.
- Visible emotion.
- Dialogue.

## Rationale

Moment anchors help a Beat become vivid, but they do not define story authority. They should not replace Story Templates or Beat Plan structure.

## Consequences

- Script drafting can use moment anchors to avoid summary-only storytelling.
- Storyboard Shots can translate the anchors into location, blocking, gesture, expression, and sound.
- Utility sequences do not need moment anchors unless they are meant to feel like a lived moment.
- Provider Export should preserve moment anchors when they are part of the approved script or storyboard.
