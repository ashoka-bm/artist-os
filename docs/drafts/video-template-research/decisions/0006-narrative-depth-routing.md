# Draft Decision 0006: Narrative Depth Routing

Status: accepted in draft.

Date: 2026-06-26

## Decision

Not every video output requires a full Story Template.

Every video output needs a purpose and payoff, but Artist OS should route video requests by **Narrative Depth**:

- `full_story`: requires a Story Template.
- `micro_journey`: requires a Micro-Journey Template.
- `utility_sequence`: requires a Format Template, sequence plan, or asset-purpose brief, but not a Story Template.

## Narrative Depth Levels

### Full Story

Use when the output needs emotional, narrative, rhetorical, or conceptual movement.

Examples: personal story, documentary mini-arc, creator lesson, reframe explainer, cinematic sketch, transformation story, problem/solution video.

### Micro-Journey

Use when the output is functional, promotional, social, or showcase-led but still needs a hook and payoff.

Examples: Instagram influencer clip, unboxing, product reveal, fashion fit check, UGC testimonial, quick before/after demo, day-in-the-life moment.

### Utility Sequence

Use when the output is an asset or supporting clip rather than a self-contained audience journey.

Examples: title card, background b-roll loop, product spin, camera-control test, style sample, transition plate, atmospheric insert.

## Rationale

Forcing a full Story Template onto every short video would make simple clips bloated. But treating social/product clips as mere sequences would make them empty. Narrative Depth lets Artist OS scale structure to the job.

## Consequences

- Story Template is required only for `full_story`.
- Micro-Journey Template is required for `micro_journey`.
- Utility sequences still need purpose, role, constraints, and success criteria.
- Workflow Scale Routing or Video Medium Plan should record narrative depth.
- Review should flag mismatches, such as a product reveal with no payoff or a full story reduced to a shot sequence.
