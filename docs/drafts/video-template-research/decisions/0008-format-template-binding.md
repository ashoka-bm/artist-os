# Draft Decision 0008: Format Template Binding

Status: accepted in draft.

Date: 2026-06-26

## Decision

Format Intent is captured early, the likely Format Template is recommended during Medium Output Shape Recommendation, and the binding Format Template is selected inside the Video Medium Plan.

## Rationale

The artist's early format preference should shape the conversation, but the final format must fit Artist Meaning, Narrative Depth, Story Template or Micro-Journey Template, audio posture, visual requirements, and production constraints.

The Video Medium Plan owns video-specific translation, so it is the right authority for the binding Format Template.

## Consequences

- Orientation can capture "short video," "talking head," "unboxing," "motion graphics," or similar format intent.
- Medium Output Shape Recommendation can confirm whether that intent still fits.
- Video Medium Plan records the binding Format Template.
- Storyboard, script, hook posture, speaker posture, and provider export must follow the binding Format Template unless revised through an explicit gate.
