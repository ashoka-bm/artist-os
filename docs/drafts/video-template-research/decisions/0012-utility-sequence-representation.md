# Draft Decision 0012: Utility Sequence Representation

Status: accepted in draft.

Date: 2026-06-26

## Decision

Utility sequences should not use Story Templates or Micro-Journey Templates.

They should use an **Asset Purpose Brief** or utility sequence plan that records:

- role in the larger video,
- subject,
- visual purpose,
- duration,
- motion,
- style constraints,
- success criteria,
- and where the output will be used.

## Rationale

Utility sequences are supporting assets. They still need purpose and payoff, but their payoff can be functional rather than narrative.

Examples of functional payoff:

- establish setting,
- show texture,
- bridge two scenes,
- create visual rhythm,
- hold attention under narration,
- test camera control,
- confirm style or reference continuity.

## Consequences

- Utility sequences avoid unnecessary story machinery.
- Review can still reject purposeless b-roll or tests.
- Provider exports can receive clear constraints without inventing narrative.
- Video Medium Plan can use utility sequences as parts of a larger story, micro-journey, or production package.
