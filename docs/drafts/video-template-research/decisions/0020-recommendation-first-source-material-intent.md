# Draft Decision 0020: Recommendation-First Source Material Intent

## Status

Draft, from grilling session.

## Decision

During Video Orientation, Artist OS should recommend a provisional Source Material Intent based on the Reference and early Artist Meaning instead of asking the artist to choose from a neutral menu.

The recommendation should say which source-material layers the video likely needs:

- video clips only,
- video clips plus voice-over,
- video clips plus music or sound,
- video clips plus voice-over plus music or sound.

The artist can accept or redirect the recommendation. The choice is directional, not final. The Video Medium Plan may revise it after storyboard planning, Audio Posture, script/dialogue needs, and pacing needs are clearer.

## Rationale

Artists often know they want a video before they know the production layer stack. A recommendation-first question gives useful direction without forcing premature finality. It also keeps Artist OS from silently planning a video-only storyboard when the likely output needs narration, dialogue, or music-led pacing.

## Consequences

- Video Orientation should include a short boundary statement: the current funnel creates source material for later editor assembly.
- The conductor should recommend Source Material Intent when enough Reference or Artist Meaning signal exists.
- The recommendation is not provider selection. Seedance, Suno, and ElevenLabs remain downstream provider routes.
- The later Video Medium Plan should record whether Source Material Intent was accepted, revised, deferred, or superseded.

## Open Questions

- Should Source Material Intent live in the Video Medium Plan schema, the Video Format Recommendation block, or a new post-storyboard production plan?
- What exact wording should the conductor use when confidence is low?
- Should the system ever skip this when the user explicitly asks only for a storyboard?
