# Draft Decision 0001: Format Intent Is A Strong Preference

Status: accepted in draft.

Date: 2026-06-26

## Decision

When the artist says they want a specific output shape, such as "short video," Artist OS captures it immediately as **Format Intent**. It is a strong preference and planning constraint, not an irreversible lock.

Format Intent becomes binding only after Medium Output Shape Recommendation confirms that the selected story movement fits the requested output shape.

## Rationale

The artist should be able to state the intended format early. That intent matters for scope, audience, pacing, and Decision Interview questions.

But story movement still belongs in the Beat Plan. If format is locked too early, platform convenience can override Artist Meaning or force the wrong story shape.

## Consequences

- Orientation and Meaning Interview can capture rough format intent.
- Beat Plan can use the intent for scale without surrendering story authority.
- Medium Output Shape Recommendation becomes the confirmation point.
- Video Medium Plan selects the binding Format Template.
- Provider Export remains downstream of storyboard approval.
