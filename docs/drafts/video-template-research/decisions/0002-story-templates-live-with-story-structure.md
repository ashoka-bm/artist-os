# Draft Decision 0002: Story Templates Live With Story Structure

Status: accepted in draft.

Date: 2026-06-26

## Decision

Reusable story templates belong with Story Structure, not in a separate video-only story authority.

Video-specific audience conventions, platform habits, and containers belong in Format Templates, Cultural Format Structure, direction notes, or provider exports depending on their authority.

## Rationale

A story movement such as "situation -> desire -> conflict -> change -> result" can become a short video, speech, essay, documentary segment, animation, or product story. The medium should not own the story's authority.

Video still needs format decisions, but those decisions should translate the selected story movement rather than define it.

## Consequences

- Beat Plan can select or adapt reusable story movement before Video Medium Plan exists.
- Video Medium Plan selects the binding Format Template and translates the story into sequences, scenes, shots, audio posture, and style.
- Hook tactics stay as Direction Notes unless they define a full story movement.
- Provider export rules remain downstream and cannot create a separate story authority.
