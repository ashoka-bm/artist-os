The numbered columns show when each track enters the shared Artist OS pipeline. Badges identify the main gate, reviewer, or record created at that point.

Hard boundary: provider-backed generation, uploads, cost-bearing actions, text Draft Generation Approval, blocking-finding waivers, and acceptance decisions all require explicit artist approval.

Reviewer Timing

Beat ReviewerBefore Story Approval for multi-beat, sequence, lyric-bearing, scene, arc, or ambiguous plans.
Story CriticOwns Story Approval; checks Beat Plan authority, scale, tension, and meaning preservation.
Medium CriticArt, Video, Sound, or Writing Critic reviews the draft brief before Brief Approval.
Prompt CriticReviews Prompt Plans, Text Generation Plans, or branch sets after Brief Approval.
Output CriticReviews a fixed Output Record before Output Acceptance.
Support ReviewersLong-Work, Illustration, and Mixed-Media reviewers enter only when those routes activate.
