# Manifesto

**Entry id**: `manifesto`

**Scope**: A declarative text states a belief system, rejects an existing condition, and calls readers toward a different future.

**Structure grounding**: Manifesto rhetoric and movement-declaration convention.

**Grounding status**: `researched`

**Grounding tier**: `craft_authority`

**Use when**: The piece should define principles, draw a line, create alignment, and make neutrality difficult.

**Audience promise**: The reader will know what the writer or group stands for, what they reject, and what future they are calling into being.

**Audience Hook logic**: Open with a charged declaration, refusal, indictment, or future claim that immediately clarifies stakes.

**Expected parts**:

| Part | Function | Default policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Declaration | States the central belief or refusal. | Required |
| Problem or enemy | Names the condition, habit, system, or assumption being opposed. | Required |
| Principles | Defines what the manifesto stands for. | Required |
| Stakes | Explains why the declaration matters. | Required |
| Call | Invites commitment, action, or allegiance. | Required |
| Future image | Shows what the new condition could make possible. | Required |

**Turn or payoff behavior**: The payoff should convert belief into alignment or action, ending with force rather than nuance alone.

**Pacing norms**:

- Use direct language.
- Keep principles memorable and distinct.
- Build intensity through accumulation.
- Do not bury the call.

**Required decisions**:

- Who is speaking, and on whose behalf?
- What is being rejected?
- What principles are non-negotiable?
- What action or allegiance is requested?

**Common failure modes**:

- The manifesto is vague inspiration without a line.
- The piece only complains and never defines principles, call, or future vision.
- The enemy is too broad to create focus.
- Principles repeat each other.
- The call is absent or timid.

**Adaptation questions**:

- What must this manifesto make impossible to ignore?
- What does the speaker refuse?
- What future does it ask the reader to join?
- How much nuance can remain without weakening force?

**Recommended Stewardship Views**:

- `argument_map`
- `evidence_stakes_map`
- `promise_payoff`
