# Scene Card

**Entry id**: `scene_card`

**Scope**: A compact planning artifact for one prose scene inside a chapter or sequence.

**Structure grounding**: Scene goal, conflict, turn, and ending-state craft convention.

**Grounding status**: `provisional`

**Grounding tier**: `craft_authority`

**Use when**: A long-text part needs scene-level planning before drafting or revision.

When used internally for cumulative work, this may appear as a `scene_brief` or `scene_stewardship_view`; Long-Work Stewardship remains the continuity authority.

**Audience promise**: The scene will do one meaningful thing and change the story state.

**Audience Hook logic**: Enter near active pressure: desire, danger, obstacle, intimacy, concealment, decision, discovery, or contradiction.

**Expected parts**:

| Part | Function | Default policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scene frame | Identifies POV, location, timeline, and containing chapter or sequence. | Required |
| Goal | Names what the active character or scene pressure seeks. | Required |
| Conflict | Names resistance, obstacle, withholding, or cost. | Required |
| Turn | Captures the change in information, relationship, value, or action. | Required |
| Ending state | States what is different after the scene. | Required |
| Continuity touchpoints | Names rules, promises, motifs, or prior parts that constrain the scene. | Required for cumulative work |

**Turn or payoff behavior**: The scene pays off when its ending state creates a necessary consequence for a later scene or chapter.

**Pacing norms**:

- Keep the card compact enough to guide drafting.
- Avoid packing multiple unrelated turns into one scene.
- Let consequence, not explanation, justify the scene.
- Use adjacent scenes to vary pressure and rhythm.

**Required decisions**:

- Whose pressure organizes the scene?
- What changes by the end?
- What continuity must be preserved?
- What unresolved thread is opened, advanced, or resolved?

**Common failure modes**:

- The scene card summarizes content but lacks a turn.
- Conflict is vague or only internal.
- The ending state does not create consequence.
- Continuity touchpoints are invented in the card instead of read from approved continuity or routed as proposed updates.

**Adaptation questions**:

- Can this scene merge with another scene?
- What would be lost if the scene were removed?
- Is the turn emotional, informational, relational, or causal?
- What does this scene require from previous parts?

**Recommended Stewardship Views**:

- `scene_sheet`
- `dependency_map`
- `open_thread_list`
