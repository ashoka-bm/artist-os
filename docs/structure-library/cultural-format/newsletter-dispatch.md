# Newsletter Dispatch

**Entry id**: `newsletter_dispatch`

**Scope**: A recurring written dispatch combines a recognizable voice, timely context, one or more observations, and a closing handoff to the reader.

**Structure grounding**: Platform-era newsletter and subscriber dispatch pattern.

**Grounding status**: `provisional`

**Grounding tier**: `platform_pattern_evidence`

**Use when**: The output should feel like an installment from a trusted writer, not a standalone article stripped of relationship.

**Audience promise**: The reader will receive a timely, voiced, curated, or reflective update worth returning to.

**Audience Hook logic**: Open with a timely note, personal observation, recurring concern, question, or compact preview that establishes why this dispatch matters now.

**Expected parts**:

| Part | Function | Default policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dispatch opening | Re-establishes voice, moment, or occasion. | Required |
| Main thread | Carries the central observation, essay, update, or argument. | Required |
| Supporting items | Adds links, notes, recommendations, or secondary observations. | Mergeable |
| Reader relevance | Makes clear why subscribers should care. | Required |
| Signoff or ask | Closes with continuity, invitation, or next touchpoint. | Required |

**Turn or payoff behavior**: The payoff should strengthen continuity with the reader: a completed thought plus a reason to keep the relationship alive.

**Pacing norms**:

- Establish the occasion before diving into material.
- Keep sections modular when multiple items appear.
- Preserve voice without delaying the main value.
- End with a light handoff, not a hard sales close unless the brief asks for it.

**Required decisions**:

- Is this essay-led, link-led, update-led, or reflection-led?
- What recurring relationship does the writer have with the reader?
- What belongs in the main thread versus supporting items?
- What should the reader do, expect, or hold after closing?

**Common failure modes**:

- The dispatch reads like a generic blog post.
- The opening is personal but unrelated to the value.
- Curated items lack a frame.
- Supporting items become the whole value, turning the dispatch into an unacknowledged curated roundup.
- The signoff is perfunctory or overly promotional.

**Adaptation questions**:

- Why is this arriving now?
- What relationship does the piece assume with the reader?
- What is the one main value of this installment?
- What continuity should carry into the next dispatch?

**Recommended Stewardship Views**:

- `section_outline`
- `promise_payoff`
- `open_threads`
