# Teardown Analysis

**Entry id**: `teardown_analysis`

**Scope**: A close analysis breaks down an example, artifact, launch, text, design, campaign, or decision to extract transferable lessons.

**Structure grounding**: Professional teardown, critique, and case-analysis pattern.

**Grounding status**: `provisional`

**Grounding tier**: `platform_pattern_evidence`

**Use when**: The piece should teach by dissecting a concrete object rather than explaining a topic in general.

**Audience promise**: The reader will see how the example works, why it succeeds or fails, and what lesson transfers elsewhere.

**Audience Hook logic**: Open with the object under inspection and the reason it is worth studying: surprising success, failure, craft move, hidden mechanism, or mistake.

**Expected parts**:

| Part | Function | Default policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Object frame | Identifies the thing being analyzed and why it matters. | Required |
| Criteria | Defines what success, failure, or craft quality means here. | Required |
| Breakdown | Examines parts, choices, sequence, or mechanics. | Required |
| Diagnosis | Explains what causes the result. | Required |
| Transferable lesson | Converts the teardown into usable principle. | Required |
| Caveat | Names context that may not transfer. | Required when advice follows |

**Turn or payoff behavior**: The payoff should move from "what happened here" to "what this teaches elsewhere."

**Pacing norms**:

- Show the object before abstracting from it.
- Keep criteria visible throughout the breakdown.
- Move from observation to diagnosis to lesson.
- Do not let admiration or criticism replace analysis.

**Required decisions**:

- What object is being inspected?
- What criteria will judge it?
- Which parts need close attention?
- What lesson transfers, and what does not?

**Common failure modes**:

- The teardown becomes a review without transferable insight.
- Criteria are unstated, so judgment feels arbitrary.
- The analysis summarizes surface features.
- The lesson ignores context.

**Adaptation questions**:

- Why this example?
- What would a casual viewer miss?
- What mechanism explains the result?
- What can the reader borrow responsibly?

**Recommended Stewardship Views**:

- `scene_evidence_map`
- `argument_map`
- `risk_caveat_map`
