# Trend Analysis

**Entry id**: `trend_analysis`

**Scope**: An analytical piece identifies a shift, explains why it is happening, and clarifies what it may change.

**Structure grounding**: Business, cultural, and platform-era analysis pattern.

**Grounding status**: `provisional`

**Grounding tier**: `platform_pattern_evidence`

**Use when**: The piece should help readers understand a pattern over time rather than a single event, opinion, or how-to.

**Audience promise**: The reader will understand what is changing, why now, who is affected, and what to watch next.

**Audience Hook logic**: Start with a visible signal, surprising data point, repeated behavior, contradiction, or before/after contrast that suggests a larger shift.

**Expected parts**:

| Part | Function | Default policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signal | Shows the observable evidence that a trend may exist. | Required |
| Trend claim | Names the shift clearly. | Required |
| Drivers | Explains forces causing or accelerating the shift. | Required |
| Stakes | Identifies who benefits, loses, adapts, or misunderstands. | Required |
| Counter-signals | Notes limits, uncertainty, or opposing evidence. | Required when credibility matters |
| Watch points | Leaves the reader with markers for what happens next. | Required |

**Turn or payoff behavior**: The payoff should convert scattered signals into a usable read on the future without overstating certainty.

**Pacing norms**:

- Separate evidence from interpretation.
- Explain why now before projecting too far.
- Include limits before the conclusion feels deterministic.
- End with concrete watch points.

**Required decisions**:

- What evidence makes this more than anecdote?
- What is the actual trend claim?
- What forces are driving it?
- What would disprove or soften the claim?
- What should the reader monitor?

**Common failure modes**:

- The piece treats one example as a trend.
- The trend label is catchy but vague.
- Drivers are asserted without mechanism.
- The ending predicts instead of giving watch points.

**Adaptation questions**:

- What repeated signal triggered the analysis?
- What has changed from the prior state?
- Who experiences the shift first?
- What uncertainty should remain visible?
