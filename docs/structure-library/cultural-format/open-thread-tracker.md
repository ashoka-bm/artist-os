# Open Thread Tracker

**Entry id**: `open_thread_tracker`

**Scope**: A readable view for promises, questions, mysteries, withheld information, unresolved choices, and continuity obligations that remain open across parts.

**Structure grounding**: Open-thread, promise, payoff, and unresolved-question tracking convention.

**Grounding status**: `provisional`

**Grounding tier**: `craft_authority`

**Use when**: A cumulative work has reader promises or continuity obligations that must not be forgotten.

**Authority mode**: Stewardship View first; CFS when delivered as an open-thread tracking document.

**Audience promise**: Opened expectations will be intentionally advanced, resolved, transformed, or left open by design.

**Audience Hook logic**: Start from the live question or promise that keeps the reader carrying attention forward.

**Expected parts**:

| Part | Function | Default policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thread | Names the open promise, question, mystery, obligation, or contradiction. | Required |
| Opened in | Identifies the Long-Work Part or medium section where it opens. | Required |
| Promise | States what the reader is implicitly owed. | Required |
| Must resolve by | Names the latest acceptable part, checkpoint, or completion boundary. | Required when owed |
| Status | Displays open, advanced, resolved, deferred, intentionally unresolved, or at-risk state from Long-Work Stewardship and reviews. | Required |
| Authority | Names whether resolution depends on Medium Plan, Beat Plan, continuity rule, or artist confirmation. | Required |

**Turn or payoff behavior**: The thread pays off when the work fulfills, revises, inverts, or explicitly refuses the promise with reader-legible intent.

**Pacing norms**:

- Make the promise concrete enough to track.
- Distinguish suspense from accidental neglect.
- Review threads at checkpoints and before completion.
- Do not resolve threads earlier than their assigned role allows.
- Do not open or close a thread from this view without the governing Beat Plan, Medium Plan, continuity rule, or artist-confirmation path.

**Required decisions**:

- What promise was opened?
- What authority governs its resolution?
- What is the latest safe resolution point?
- What happens if the thread changes during drafting?

**Common failure modes**:

- Every loose idea becomes an open thread.
- Threads are resolved without checking Beat Plan or Medium Plan intent.
- The tracker becomes a separate continuity database.
- Status is updated without evidence from parts or approved plans.

**Adaptation questions**:

- Is this a reader promise, continuity rule, motif, or note?
- What would make the promise feel paid off?
- Should it remain unresolved intentionally?
- What checkpoint should review it?

**Recommended Stewardship Views**:

- `open_thread_list`
- `promise_payoff`
- `pre_completion_checkpoint`
