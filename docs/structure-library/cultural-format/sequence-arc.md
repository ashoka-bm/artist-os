# Sequence Arc

**Entry id**: `sequence_arc`

**Scope**: A linked run of scenes or chapters with a shared objective, escalation pattern, turn, and consequence.

**Structure grounding**: Fiction sequence, escalation, midpoint or reversal, and consequence craft convention.

**Grounding status**: `provisional`

**Grounding tier**: `craft_authority`

**Use when**: Several chapters or scenes need to operate as one larger movement inside a novel, novella, or serial.

Sequence authority comes from the Beat Plan, Text Medium Plan, and Long-Work Stewardship part plan; this entry describes the readable sequence grammar.

**Audience promise**: The sequence will build pressure across parts and leave the story materially changed.

**Audience Hook logic**: Open with a sequence-level objective, problem, mystery, threat, temptation, or pursuit that cannot be satisfied in one scene.

**Expected parts**:

| Part | Function | Default policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sequence objective | Names what the linked parts are trying to achieve or test. | Required |
| Starting state | Captures the state before escalation begins. | Required |
| Escalation path | Shows how pressure increases across parts. | Required |
| Reversal or hinge | Turns the sequence into new knowledge, cost, or direction. | Required |
| Consequence | Names the changed state that affects later parts. | Required |
| Dependent parts | Lists chapters or scenes included in the sequence. | Required |

**Turn or payoff behavior**: The sequence pays off when the reversal or consequence changes the next part's options, stakes, or self-understanding.

**Pacing norms**:

- Each included part should add pressure, not repeat it.
- Keep the sequence objective legible across interruptions.
- Use the hinge to prevent a flat middle.
- Let the consequence feed later Long-Work Parts.

**Required decisions**:

- Which parts belong to the sequence?
- What shared objective or pressure binds them?
- Where does the sequence turn?
- Which continuity rules or checkpoints protect it?

**Common failure modes**:

- The sequence is only a group of adjacent chapters.
- Escalation repeats the same pressure.
- The hinge is missing or arrives without setup.
- Consequence is local but not cumulative.

**Adaptation questions**:

- Does the sequence need a checkpoint before continuing?
- Which part is the calibration or hinge part?
- What should remain unresolved at sequence end?
- What continuity update would change the sequence authority?

**Recommended Stewardship Views**:

- `sequence_tracker`
- `act_tracker`
- `checkpoint_plan`
