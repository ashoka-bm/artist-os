# Character Brief

**Entry id**: `character_brief`

**Scope**: A readable character projection that captures dramatic function, desire, contradiction, arc state, voice, relationships, and verified continuity.

**Structure grounding**: Character sheet, dramatic function, arc, relationship, voice, and continuity-log craft convention.

**Grounding status**: `provisional`

**Grounding tier**: `craft_authority`

**Use when**: A long-text project needs a compact character view for planning, drafting, or continuity review.

**Authority mode**: Stewardship View first; CFS when delivered as a character document. Internally, prefer `character_continuity_view`.

**Audience promise**: The character will feel continuous, pressured, and dramatically useful across parts.

**Audience Hook logic**: Begin with the character's active pressure: what they want, fear, hide, misunderstand, refuse, or keep repeating.

**Expected parts**:

| Part | Function | Default policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity | Names role, first appearance, POV status, and story function. | Required |
| Dramatic pressure | Captures wants, needs, fears, false belief, and contradiction. | Required |
| Arc states | Tracks starting, middle, ending, and proof-of-change states. | Required when arc matters |
| Relationships | Captures ally, rival, romantic, family, and power dynamics. | Required when relational pressure matters |
| Voice | Captures diction, rhythm, avoided speech, and repeated habits. | Required for drafting |
| Continuity projection | Displays approved continuity, source evidence, proposed updates, and unresolved conflicts from Long-Work Stewardship. | Required for cumulative work |

**Turn or payoff behavior**: The brief pays off when later chapters can preserve or intentionally change the character through approved continuity updates.

**Pacing norms**:

- Keep the brief usable for drafting rather than exhaustive.
- Separate planned arc from verified draft discoveries.
- Treat voice as a constraint only when approved or evidenced.
- Keep contradictions active instead of smoothing them away.
- Do not add or revise character canon from this view; route changes through proposed continuity updates.

**Required decisions**:

- Which character facts are approved continuity rules?
- Which are candidate ideas or proposed updates?
- What change requires artist confirmation or story approval?
- Which chapters or scenes depend on this character state?

**Common failure modes**:

- The brief becomes a separate story bible.
- Character changes are inferred from drafts without verification.
- Voice rules are too generic to guide prose.
- Arc states contradict the Beat Plan or Medium Plan.

**Adaptation questions**:

- What must remain stable across chapters?
- What is allowed to change, and when?
- What source evidence supports each continuity point?
- What unresolved contradiction creates useful pressure?

**Recommended Stewardship Views**:

- `character_continuity_view`
- `relationship_map`
- `voice_continuity_view`
