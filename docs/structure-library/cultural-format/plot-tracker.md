# Plot Tracker

**Entry id**: `plot_tracker`

**Scope**: A readable projection of premise, theme, genre promise, act structure, subplots, and open threads for a long narrative work.

**Structure grounding**: Plot tracker, act structure, genre promise, subplot, and open-thread craft convention.

**Grounding status**: `provisional`

**Grounding tier**: `craft_authority`

**Use when**: A long-text project needs one compact planning or review surface for story movement and unresolved obligations.

**Authority mode**: Stewardship View first; CFS when delivered as a readable plot tracker document.

**Audience promise**: The whole work will preserve its central promise, major turns, subplots, and unresolved threads across chapters or scenes.

**Audience Hook logic**: Start with the premise pressure and genre promise that make the plot worth tracking.

**Expected parts**:

| Part | Function | Default policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Premise | States the story situation and central pressure. | Required |
| Theme | Captures the meaning question or pressure being tested. | Required |
| Genre promise | Names the reader expectation created by genre, tone, opening, and premise. | Required |
| Story Structure / act projection | Projects major turns from the governing Story Structure, such as hook, inciting incident, midpoint, darkest point, climax, and resolution when those labels apply. | Required |
| Subplots | Displays secondary lines by owner, setup, development, payoff, and projected status. | Required when present |
| Open threads | Displays promises, deadlines, and projected resolution status. | Required |

**Turn or payoff behavior**: The plot tracker pays off by making it visible whether the ending resolves, transforms, or intentionally complicates the opening promise.

**Pacing norms**:

- Use the tracker as a view, not a replacement Beat Plan.
- Keep act labels flexible when the governing Story Structure differs.
- Review subplots and open threads before major checkpoints.
- Keep status tied to evidence from Medium Plan or Long-Work Stewardship.
- Do not revise Beat Plan, Story Structure, or continuity state from this view.

**Required decisions**:

- Which Story Structure or adapted act logic governs movement?
- Which parts are projected from the Text Medium Plan?
- Which continuity rules or proposed updates affect plot state?
- Which open threads must block completion if unresolved?

**Common failure modes**:

- The tracker becomes a competing story authority.
- Act labels override the adapted Story Structure.
- Subplots are tracked without payoff decisions.
- Open threads are closed without reader-legible payoff.

**Adaptation questions**:

- What should this view hide to stay usable?
- What must it show before drafting continues?
- Which genre promise matters most?
- What drift should trigger Long-Work Reviewer escalation?

**Recommended Stewardship Views**:

- `plot_tracker`
- `act_tracker`
- `subplot_tracker`
- `open_thread_list`
