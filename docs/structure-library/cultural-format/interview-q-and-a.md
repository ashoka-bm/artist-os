# Interview Q&A

**Entry id**: `interview_q_and_a`

**Scope**: A question-and-answer piece presents an edited conversation with a clear frame, progression, pivots, and closing insight.

**Structure grounding**: Interview introduction, question sequence, edited answer, and closing convention.

**Grounding status**: `provisional`

**Grounding tier**: `craft_authority`

**Use when**: The audience value comes from a subject's voice, expertise, memory, or point of view rather than from a fully narrated article.

**Audience promise**: The reader will hear the subject think, speak, remember, explain, or reveal something through a coherent conversation.

**Audience Hook logic**: Open with why this person and this conversation matter now, or with the tension/question the exchange will illuminate.

**Expected parts**:

| Part | Function | Default policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Interview frame | Introduces the subject, occasion, stakes, and editing context. | Required |
| Opening question | Starts with an accessible doorway into the subject. | Required |
| Question progression | Moves through background, core issue, complication, and implication. | Required |
| Follow-up pivots | Respond to answers with deeper or sharper questions. | Required |
| Voice-preserving answers | Keeps the subject's phrasing while clarifying structure. | Required |
| Closing question / insight | Ends on reflection, implication, future, or residue. | Required |

**Turn or payoff behavior**: The turn appears when the conversation moves from prepared surface to specific insight, contradiction, admission, or changed frame.

**Pacing norms**:

- Keep the intro short but orienting.
- Order questions for reader comprehension, not recording chronology.
- Trim repetition without flattening voice.
- Use follow-ups to create movement.

**Required decisions**:

- Why this subject now?
- What is the conversation's central question?
- How much editing is allowed?
- Which answers need context or compression?
- What final question leaves meaning rather than abruptness?

**Common failure modes**:

- The Q&A reads like a transcript dump.
- Questions do not build on each other.
- The interviewer over-explains.
- Editing removes the subject's voice.
- The ending stops instead of lands.

**Adaptation questions**:

- What does the reader want from this person's voice?
- What sequence of questions creates discovery?
- Where should the interviewer challenge, clarify, or pivot?
- Which answer carries the piece's central value?
- What should the last exchange leave open or resolved?

**Recommended Stewardship Views**:

- `question_arc`
- `voice_integrity`
- `open_threads`
