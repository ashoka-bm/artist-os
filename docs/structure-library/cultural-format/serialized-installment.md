# Serialized Installment

**Entry id**: `serialized_installment`

**Scope**: A written installment advances a larger ongoing work while giving the reader a satisfying unit of movement.

**Structure grounding**: Serialized fiction, newsletter serial, and episodic publishing pattern.

**Grounding status**: `provisional`

**Grounding tier**: `platform_pattern_evidence`

**Use when**: The output belongs to a continuing sequence and must balance recap, new development, payoff, and next promise.

For cumulative long-form projects, Long-Work Stewardship remains the continuity authority; this entry describes installment grammar, not a separate continuity record.

Continuity facts, open-thread status, canon changes, drift, and proposed updates must come from Long-Work Stewardship, Output Records, Review Records, or Gate Decisions. The installment may express or advance that state; it must not track or mutate it independently.

**Audience promise**: The reader will receive a meaningful new movement in the larger sequence and a reason to continue.

**Audience Hook logic**: Open with a recap pressure, unresolved thread, immediate scene, question, or reversal that reconnects the reader to the serial engine.

**Expected parts**:

| Part | Function | Default policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Continuity bridge | Reorients the reader without dumping backstory. | Required |
| Installment promise | Establishes what this unit will move or reveal. | Required |
| New development | Advances plot, argument, relationship, discovery, or inquiry. | Required |
| Local payoff | Gives this installment its own satisfaction. | Required |
| Open loop | Carries tension, question, or promise forward. | Required |
| Next signal | Suggests why the next installment matters. | Mergeable |

**Turn or payoff behavior**: The installment should pay off something locally while opening or intensifying something larger.

**Pacing norms**:

- Reorient quickly.
- Avoid full recap unless the serial has been away for a long time.
- Give the installment a complete internal movement.
- End with forward pull, not arbitrary interruption.

**Required decisions**:

- What must returning readers remember?
- What can new readers infer without full explanation?
- What does this installment pay off?
- What remains open for the next unit?

**Common failure modes**:

- The installment is only setup for a later payoff.
- Recap overwhelms present movement.
- The ending is a cliffhanger without earned pressure.
- Continuity assumes too much or explains too much.

**Adaptation questions**:

- What larger promise is this installment serving?
- What local change must happen here?
- Which thread is advanced, resolved, or opened?
- What should the reader want next?

**Recommended Stewardship Views**:

- `open_threads`
- `plot_tracker`
- `promise_payoff`
