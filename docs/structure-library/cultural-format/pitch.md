# Pitch

**Entry id**: `pitch`

**Scope**: A persuasive text asks a specific audience to believe, fund, approve, buy, join, publish, or take a next step.

**Structure grounding**: Pitch, proposal, and persuasive business communication convention.

**Grounding status**: `researched`

**Grounding tier**: `craft_authority`

**Use when**: The output must convert attention into a concrete decision rather than merely explain or inspire.

**Audience promise**: The reader will understand the opportunity, why it matters, why this solution or creator can deliver, and what decision is being requested.

**Audience Hook logic**: Open with the problem, opportunity, audience pain, timely shift, or sharp outcome that makes the ask worth hearing.

**Expected parts**:

| Part | Function | Default policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Problem or opportunity | Shows why the pitch matters. | Required |
| Insight | Reveals the non-obvious understanding behind the proposal. | Required |
| Offer or solution | States what is being proposed. | Required |
| Proof | Demonstrates credibility, traction, fit, or feasibility. | Required |
| Fit for audience | Explains why this audience should care. | Required |
| Ask | Names the concrete decision or next step. | Required |

**Turn or payoff behavior**: The payoff is decision-readiness: the reader should know what is being asked and why saying yes makes sense.

**Pacing norms**:

- Get to the ask before the reader loses the thread.
- Keep proof tied to the decision.
- Make audience fit explicit.
- Avoid hiding risk; handle it briefly and concretely.

**Required decisions**:

- Who is deciding?
- What exactly are they being asked to do?
- What proof matters to this audience?
- What objection must be neutralized?

**Common failure modes**:

- The pitch explains the idea but never asks.
- The audience fit is assumed.
- Proof is impressive but irrelevant.
- The problem is generic, so the solution feels optional.

**Adaptation questions**:

- What decision should happen after reading?
- What does this audience already value?
- What is the strongest reason to say no?
- What proof makes the next step feel reasonable?

**Recommended Stewardship Views**:

- `argument_map`
- `decision_tree`
- `risk_caveat_map`
