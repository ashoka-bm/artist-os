# Chapter

**Entry id**: `chapter`

**Scope**: A long-fiction chapter that carries a local promise, advances cumulative story pressure, and leaves the reader in a changed state.

**Structure grounding**: Chapter brief, scene list, chapter purpose, and synthesis-input craft convention.

**Grounding status**: `provisional`

**Grounding tier**: `craft_authority`

**Use when**: The accepted text part is one chapter inside a cumulative long-text work.

**Audience promise**: The chapter will reward the reader with movement: plot, character, world, revelation, escalation, intimacy, or consequence.

**Audience Hook logic**: Open with a local pressure, image, question, voice, task, conflict, or aftermath that justifies entering this chapter now.

**Expected parts**:

| Part | Function | Default policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chapter metadata | Identifies POV, location, timeline, draft phase, and status. | Required |
| Chapter purpose | Names plot, character, world, and reader-promise functions. | Required |
| Scene list | Breaks the chapter into goal, conflict, turn, and ending state. | Required |
| Summary | Captures what changes in the chapter. | Required |
| Synthesis input | Provides a compact drafting packet for the chapter. | Mergeable |
| Proposed or approved continuity updates | Lists draft-discovered changes that remain proposed until approved, or approved changes already reflected in Long-Work Stewardship. | Required after drafting |
| Audit notes | Names revision or continuity issues. | Optional |

**Turn or payoff behavior**: The chapter should end with a changed state, sharpened question, consequence, reveal, or handoff into the next cumulative part.

**Pacing norms**:

- Give each chapter one dominant job even when it contains multiple scenes.
- Use scene turns to create chapter-level movement.
- Avoid chapters that only transfer information.
- Preserve unresolved promises unless the chapter is assigned to resolve them.
- Treat draft-discovered continuity changes as proposed until the required approval path accepts them.

**Required decisions**:

- What is this chapter's part job in Long-Work Stewardship?
- What should the reader know, feel, expect, or fear by the end?
- Which continuity rules apply?
- What new updates require verification before becoming active continuity?

**Common failure modes**:

- The chapter brief duplicates the full outline.
- The chapter advances plot but breaks voice, timeline, or character continuity.
- New canon is silently accepted without verification.
- The ending state does not differ from the opening state.

**Adaptation questions**:

- Which Beat or Long-Work Part does this chapter fulfill?
- What is the chapter's required turn?
- What must not resolve yet?
- What checkpoint should follow if this is an early or risky chapter?

**Recommended Stewardship Views**:

- `chapter_brief`
- `scene_list`
- `continuity_update_queue`
- `chapter_checkpoint`
