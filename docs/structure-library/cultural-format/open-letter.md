# Open Letter

**Entry id**: `open_letter`

**Scope**: A public letter addresses a named person, group, institution, or community while also speaking to observers.

**Structure grounding**: Open letter and public appeal convention.

**Grounding status**: `researched`

**Grounding tier**: `craft_authority`

**Use when**: The piece needs direct address, public accountability, moral pressure, appeal, or witness.

**Audience promise**: The reader will understand who is being addressed, what is at stake, and what response or recognition is being demanded.

**Audience Hook logic**: Open with direct address and a clear occasion, wound, appeal, gratitude, warning, or demand.

**Expected parts**:

| Part | Function | Default policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Addressee | Identifies who is being addressed. | Required |
| Occasion | Explains why the letter is public and timely. | Required |
| Claim or appeal | States the grievance, request, warning, or recognition. | Required |
| Evidence or witness | Grounds the appeal in experience, facts, or consequence. | Required |
| Public stakes | Shows why observers should care. | Required |
| Ask or closing charge | Names the desired response or leaves accountable pressure. | Required |

**Turn or payoff behavior**: The payoff should make avoidance harder for the addressee and clarify the public stakes for everyone else.

**Pacing norms**:

- Maintain direct address.
- Balance intimacy with public legibility.
- Let evidence support pressure.
- End with a clear ask or charged final recognition.

**Required decisions**:

- Who is the letter addressed to?
- Why must it be public?
- What is being asked, named, or refused?
- What evidence makes the appeal accountable?

**Common failure modes**:

- The letter forgets its addressee and becomes an essay.
- The public stakes are unclear.
- The ask is vague.
- The tone escalates without evidence.

**Adaptation questions**:

- What can only be said through direct address?
- Who else is meant to witness the letter?
- What response would count as meaningful?
- What should the addressee be unable to ignore?

**Recommended Stewardship Views**:

- `argument_map`
