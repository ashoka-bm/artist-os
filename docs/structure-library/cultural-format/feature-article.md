# Feature Article

**Entry id**: `feature_article`

**Scope**: A reported, researched, or deeply observed article uses scene, character, evidence, and context to reveal the meaning of a subject.

**Structure grounding**: Feature article lede, nut graf, development, and kicker convention.

**Grounding status**: `researched`

**Grounding tier**: `craft_authority`

**Use when**: The accepted shape needs narrative entry, human texture, context, development, and a meaningful close rather than direct advice or pure argument.

**Audience promise**: The reader will enter through a vivid doorway and leave understanding why the subject matters beyond the opening scene.

**Audience Hook logic**: Start with a scene, person, image, tension, question, or surprising detail that embodies the larger meaning.

**Expected parts**:

| Part | Function | Default policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lede | Draws the reader into the subject through scene, image, person, or tension. | Required |
| Nut / theme | Connects the lede to the larger meaning and story focus. | Required unless the narrative drive clearly carries it |
| Context | Gives the reader enough background to understand stakes. | Required |
| Development | Builds the subject through scenes, evidence, examples, reporting, or reflection. | Required |
| Turn or complication | Prevents the piece from staying on one explanatory plane. | Required |
| Kicker / close | Lands the meaning through echo, image, consequence, or changed attention. | Required |

**Turn or payoff behavior**: The close should make the lede feel necessary, not decorative. The reader should feel the subject has opened outward.

**Pacing norms**:

- Let the lede breathe, but do not strand the reader.
- Give the nut or focus early enough to orient the article.
- Alternate texture and explanation.
- Use the turn to deepen the theme, not merely add another fact.

**Required decisions**:

- What scene, person, object, or tension carries the lede?
- What is the article's theme statement or focus?
- What context does the reader need before the middle deepens?
- What turn complicates the subject?
- Should the close echo the lede, widen the frame, or leave residue?

**Common failure modes**:

- The lede is vivid but disconnected from the article.
- The nut states topic but not meaning.
- The middle becomes a pile of material.
- The turn adds variety without changing understanding.
- The kicker strains for cleverness instead of payoff.

**Adaptation questions**:

- What doorway best embodies the larger subject?
- What does this story mean beyond its facts?
- Where should context interrupt narrative?
- What complication keeps the piece honest?
- What image, consequence, or sentence should remain after reading?

**Recommended Stewardship Views**:

- `theme_statement`
- `scene_evidence_map`
- `promise_payoff`
