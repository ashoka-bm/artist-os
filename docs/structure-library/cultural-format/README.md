# Cultural Format Structures

Cultural Format Structure entries describe audience-facing format grammar. They help Artist OS make a Medium Plan feel culturally legible without replacing the Beat Plan or the Medium Plan's execution structure.

Read this index first, then open only the entry that matches the accepted output shape.

| Entry id | File | Use when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hook_driven_article` | [hook-driven-article.md](hook-driven-article.md) | An article needs a strong hook, promise, turn, and payoff. |
| `service_article_how_to` | [service-article-how-to.md](service-article-how-to.md) | The piece should help the reader understand, choose, fix, do, or improve something. |
| `feature_article` | [feature-article.md](feature-article.md) | The piece uses scene, character, evidence, and context to reveal a subject's meaning. |
| `op_ed` | [op-ed.md](op-ed.md) | The piece makes a timely, accountable public argument. |
| `short_story` | [short-story.md](short-story.md) | The piece needs a complete fictional pressure, turn, and residue within limited space. |
| `screenplay_scene` | [screenplay-scene.md](screenplay-scene.md) | The piece is a screen or script scene with objective, obstacle, turn, and early exit. |

## Chooser Guide

Choose by the audience-facing promise and grammar, not by deep emotional movement.

- Use `hook_driven_article` when the main format problem is earning attention and paying off a hook.
- Use `service_article_how_to` when the reader expects practical understanding, steps, decision support, caveats, or next action.
- Use `feature_article` when scene, character, observation, evidence, and context reveal why a subject matters.
- Use `op_ed` when the piece makes an arguable public claim with stakes, evidence, speaker authority, and counterargument.
- Use `short_story` when the accepted form is fictional prose that must feel like a complete story rather than an article or scene.
- Use `screenplay_scene` when the accepted form is written for visible action, objective, obstacle, subtext, turn, and early exit.

## Common Confusion Pairs

- `hook_driven_article` vs `feature_article`: choose hook-driven article when hook/promise/payoff is the main grammar; choose feature article when reported or observed texture, context, and kicker carry the form.
- `hook_driven_article` vs `service_article_how_to`: choose hook-driven article for attention-to-meaning; choose service when usefulness, steps, caveats, and next action are the reader promise.
- `op_ed` vs `hook_driven_article`: choose op-ed when the piece must make an accountable arguable claim; choose hook-driven article when the piece may explain, reflect, or report without a public argument.
- `short_story` vs `screenplay_scene`: choose short story for prose completeness and residue; choose screenplay scene for visible action, objective, obstacle, and a performable turn.
- Cultural Format Structure vs Story Structure: choose Cultural Format Structure for audience-facing form grammar. The Beat Plan's Story Structure still owns deep movement.

## Do Not Use Cultural Format Structure For

- replacing Beat Plan movement or Story Structure,
- deciding whether a work should be a single image, image series, song, video, or text,
- forcing every expected part when the adaptation policy says parts can merge, omit, or invert,
- treating platform limits as first-class Artist OS validation rules.
