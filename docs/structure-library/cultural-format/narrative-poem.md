# Narrative Poem

**Entry id**: `narrative_poem`

**Scope**: A poem carries story movement through event, image, voice, and compression while preserving poetic pressure.

**Structure grounding**: Narrative poetry craft convention.

**Grounding status**: `researched`

**Grounding tier**: `craft_authority`

**Use when**: The accepted shape is a poem that tells or implies an event, journey, memory, myth, scene sequence, or transformation.

**Audience promise**: The reader will follow a story-shaped movement while receiving the compression, sound, image, and resonance of poetry.

**Audience Hook logic**: Begin with an event, charged scene, speaker claim, mythic premise, or image that implies something has happened or is about to happen.

**Expected parts**:

| Part | Function | Default policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Narrative situation | Establishes event, speaker, subject, or occasion. | Required |
| Movement through time or change | Gives the poem progression. | Required |
| Image and sound field | Keeps the piece poem-first rather than summary. | Required |
| Pressure or complication | Creates stakes, mystery, contradiction, or consequence. | Required |
| Turn / recognition / reversal | Changes the meaning of the event or speaker's relation to it. | Required |
| Ending image or carried consequence | Gives story residue without flattening into explanation. | Required |

**Turn or payoff behavior**: The payoff should satisfy narrative expectation while preserving poetic openness, echo, or compression.

**Pacing norms**:

- Select only the story details the poem can charge.
- Let leaps and images carry time when possible.
- Keep exposition lean.
- Balance narrative clarity with poetic intensity.

**Required decisions**:

- What event or change organizes the poem?
- How much story must the reader know?
- What is gained by making this a poem instead of prose?
- Where does the event's meaning turn?
- What image carries the consequence?

**Common failure modes**:

- The piece becomes chopped-up prose.
- Story clarity disappears into private images.
- Exposition drains poetic pressure.
- The poem reports a change but does not let the reader feel it.
- The ending resolves the plot but loses resonance.

**Adaptation questions**:

- What is the smallest story the poem needs?
- Which parts of the event can be skipped, leapt, or implied?
- What repeated image or sound pattern can bind the movement?
- What should the ending answer, and what should it leave open?

**Recommended Stewardship Views**:

- `beat_map`
- `image_field`
- `open_threads` for linked poem sequences
