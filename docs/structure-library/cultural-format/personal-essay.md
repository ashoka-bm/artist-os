# Personal Essay

**Entry id**: `personal_essay`

**Scope**: A first-person prose piece uses lived experience, reflection, scene, and turn to arrive at earned meaning.

**Structure grounding**: Personal essay scene, reflection, turn, and resonance convention.

**Grounding status**: `provisional`

**Grounding tier**: `craft_authority`

**Use when**: The piece should think through an experience from the inside while giving the reader meaning beyond confession or anecdote.

**Audience promise**: The reader will be invited into a particular lived experience and leave with a larger recognition, question, or changed understanding.

**Audience Hook logic**: Begin with a charged scene, admission, image, contradiction, or question that creates intimacy and stakes without over-explaining the lesson.

**Expected parts**:

| Part | Function | Default policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intimate doorway | Opens through scene, image, confession, contradiction, or question. | Required |
| Situation | Gives enough context to understand the experience. | Required |
| Reflection thread | Shows the narrator thinking, not merely remembering. | Required |
| Pressure / complication | Introduces conflict, shame, uncertainty, cost, or contradiction. | Required |
| Turn | Changes what the narrator or reader understands. | Required |
| Larger meaning | Connects personal material to a broader human, cultural, or artistic resonance. | Required |
| Earned close | Lands with image, recognition, question, or restraint. | Required |

**Turn or payoff behavior**: The payoff should feel discovered rather than announced. The ending may clarify, complicate, or deepen the opening pressure.

**Pacing norms**:

- Move between scene and reflection.
- Do not explain the meaning before the reader feels the experience.
- Let vulnerability serve inquiry, not performance.
- Keep abstraction anchored in concrete detail.

**Required decisions**:

- What experience is the essay thinking through?
- What is the narrator's initial belief or wound?
- What complicates that belief?
- What larger meaning is earned by the particulars?
- What should remain unresolved, if anything?

**Common failure modes**:

- The piece becomes diary entry without reader-facing meaning.
- The lesson is stated too early.
- Reflection replaces scene.
- Vulnerability is used as proof instead of inquiry.
- The ending over-explains the transformation.

**Adaptation questions**:

- What is the moment the essay cannot stop returning to?
- What did the narrator think then, and what can they see now?
- What tension keeps the essay from becoming a moral?
- Where does the personal material meet a larger pattern?
- What final image or sentence can carry the meaning quietly?

**Recommended Stewardship Views**:

- `scene_reflection_balance`
- `meaning_thread`
- `promise_payoff`
